For every seed, the four arms have identical baseline checkpoint hashes, online loss histories and visible metrics. The mean recorded epoch loss decreases from 0.5602 at epoch 1 to 0.04064 at epoch 100. This is online minibatch BCE while weights are updated, not fixed-checkpoint training BCE. Training AP, AUROC and accuracy were not recorded; no train-set predictions were added.

At threshold 0.5, logistic predicts all 148 final conditions as negative (TP=0, FP=0, FN=22, TN=126). Its accuracy 0.85135 is exactly the all-negative reference, not positive-case detection. Its AUROC 0.6385 nevertheless describes continuous-score ranking, not thresholded decisions.

The fixed head's strong gate ranking does not transfer to development or final, despite low late-epoch training loss. Role prevalences also differ. These observations establish a generalization warning, not a unique causal explanation; no causal attribution to prevalence, overfitting or biological heterogeneity is established here.
