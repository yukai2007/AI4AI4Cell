\subsection*{L.1 Complete transfer of the frozen architecture roster}
The full-training study transfers every architecture selected by the fifteen historical DTI campaigns, rather than choosing architectures by their old test scores. The historical source-selection seal predates those tests. Fixed DrugBAN, released DrugBAN-Evo, harness-free, native DrugEvolve and our harness give five method labels at each of five paired fit seeds. Historical search seeds 42--46 map to fit seeds 342--346. Exact source/seed/recipe identity reduces the 25 labels to 21 actual fits and 13 distinct sources. The baseline is aliased with harness-free at search seed 44 and with all three controllers at seed 46; different fit seeds never share a checkpoint.

Each controller average combines five campaign-selected programs, each refit once at its corresponding training seed, rather than five retrainings of every selected architecture. The two planned unseen-drug contrasts use 10,000-resample paired percentile intervals and exact two-sided sign-flip tests with Holm correction. Variation combines program search and refitting on fixed test rows. The shared baseline at index 46 contributes an exact zero to both contrasts, so raw exact p-values cannot be below 0.125 and Holm-adjusted values cannot be below 0.25.

Every model uses all 50,149 original BindingDB training pairs and the same 5,604 original validation pairs. Training runs 100 epochs, batch size 64, Adam learning rate $5\times10^{-5}$, default Adam betas and epsilon, and the original sigmoid plus binary-cross-entropy objective. There is no weight decay, scheduler, automatic mixed precision, gradient clipping or score-based early stopping. The original drop-last convention trains on 50,112 rows of each deterministic epoch permutation. Each architecture receives the same fit-seed encoder and classifier tensors by explicit copying, preventing architecture-dependent random-number consumption from changing their initialization. All modules are trained end-to-end. This matches data, protected-component initialization and 78,300 optimizer updates, not parameter count or FLOPs: DrugBAN has 1.071M parameters, selected programs 1.071--1.088M and released Evo 1.281M. The fixed Evo row uses the constructor defaults of the pinned release; optional Sinkhorn and Top-K variants are not activated.

Historical search used 2,048 training, 512 development and 512 selection pairs drawn only from the original training file, with three epochs per candidate. Full refitting reincorporates all these former search-feedback rows into training; the separate original validation file now supplies checkpoint selection. No new architecture search is run.

All 100 epochs and validation prediction vectors are retained. Selection is the earliest strict maximum validation AUROC across the complete run; test scores never select a checkpoint. Original DrugBAN, graph and sequence encoders, featurization, scorer and full test rows remain pinned. The selected BAN sources often implement attention directly in their forward method, so a task loader installs those exact sources without requiring the released Evo module's optional attention helper. This compatibility change does not rewrite a selected architecture. Administrative restart can resume the same model, optimizer, permutation and random-number state; it cannot change the recipe or remove a failed seed.

The primary endpoint remains unseen-drug AUROC, with random and unseen-protein AUROC, AP, BCE, accuracy at 0.5 and MCC also retained. The tests contain 5,505/3,791/2,507 pairs. Each completed fit is source-, checkpoint- and validation-verified before its test inference. The final table requires all 25 labels, including declared aliases. The public tests were exposed in the historical study: this is a full-training transfer evaluation, not a newly blind architecture-search experiment. Its results are separate from the twelve-epoch, 8,192-row historical refit.

\subsection*{L.2 Contemporary fixed reference}
TAPB \citep{lin2025tapb} is evaluated as an additional fixed model on exactly these original training, validation and test rows. It is not another BAN-only controller. We pin its released source to ac846b1 and execute the official training epoch, including its target augmentation and masked-language-model objective. Protein features come from frozen ESM2-650M \citep{lin2023evolutionary} with a 2,000-token limit and 1,280-dimensional outputs. The MoLFormer asset supplies only a tokenizer: TAPB's 256-dimensional, three-layer drug Transformer is randomly initialized. There are 6,823,868 trainable parameters, in addition to the frozen protein encoder.

Eight target-confounder centers and the amino-acid dictionary use only the 757 unique training proteins, with seed-specific KMeans initialization and ten restarts. The supplied mean target embedding includes special tokens; the amino-acid dictionary excludes them. Validation proteins can be encoded independently by the frozen ESM model but do not enter these dictionaries. The target deletion ratio is 0.7, with one batch-shared retained-position set in the released collator; mutation probability is 0.2 and drug masking probability 0.15. The official collator aliases masked and supervised drug-token dictionaries; this behavior is preserved. Training sums the official adjusted-probability negative log likelihood and unweighted masked-token loss.

The fixed TAPB recipe is 100 epochs, batch size 60, AdamW learning rate $2\times10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$ and FP32, with no scheduler or early stopping. It uses seeds 342--346 and the earliest strict maximum validation AUROC. We replace the supplied workflow's test-derived Youden threshold with the common fixed threshold 0.5 and never select using a test set. Official collation, an optimizer step, fixed-threshold scoring and exact checkpoint restart are covered by synthetic equivalence checks.

TAPB's larger pretrained representation and different recipe make it a predictive-strength reference, not a capacity-matched estimate of the research controller's effect. Contemporary DTI methods also include CIPFN-DTI \citep{cao2025cipfn} and CMA-DTI \citep{qin2026cmadti}; they are literature context, not measured rows in our roster. Published numbers from different BindingDB partitions, cross-validation procedures or coldness definitions are not inserted into the matched table. The full-data deterministic cosine reference uses the same 50,149 training pairs, fixed five-neighbor averaging and the historical Morgan/composition feature definition.

\subsection*{L.3 Provenance and costs}
The architecture-transfer manifest binds all 25 labels, source identities, common initialization and training recipe. The evaluation plan separately binds the unchanged scorer and all three endpoints. TAPB additionally records pretrained asset revisions, training-only dictionary hashes, exact environment and its own frozen runner. All selected and fixed models retain seed-level metrics and actual fit costs. Shared pretrained feature extraction is a separate cost, not omitted task training; identical checkpoint aliases are not counted as extra fits. Search costs remain those of the historical campaigns and are not relabeled as full-data search. The two fixed seed-342 fits began before the full-roster manifest; source, data, recipe and shared-initialization compatibility were verified before their declared reuse. Wall times are observed under shared GPU scheduling, not a controlled model-speed benchmark. Administrative queue changes do not alter scientific fit commands.

\subsection*{L.4 Completed results}
All 21 enrolled BAN-family fits and five TAPB fits completed 100 epochs. The final matrix contains 75 BAN-family label/seed/endpoint records, 15 TAPB records and three deterministic cosine records; no roster label is omitted. Publication independently reproduces the unchanged scorer on all 81 unique test prediction vectors and all 26 selected validation vectors, verifies their original row order and labels, and checks every source, seed, selection and checkpoint binding before expanding the declared aliases.

Our harness-selected architectures achieve mean AUROC $0.9474/0.9330/0.8534$ on random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein, the highest mean in the five-method BAN-family roster. TAPB achieves $0.9545/0.9328/0.8868$: it is stronger on random and unseen-protein tests, while the unseen-drug point estimates are nearly tied. The prespecified unseen-drug improvement over native DrugEvolve is $0.00913$ (paired percentile interval $[0.00214,0.01613]$, exact $p=0.125$); over harness-free it is $0.00506$ ($[-0.00097,0.01032]$, exact $p=0.25$). Both Holm-adjusted values are $0.25$. Thus the complete refits show a positive descriptive transfer effect for the selected architectures, not statistically established controller superiority. \subsection*{L.5 Measured full-fit costs}
Counting each actual fit once, the 21 BAN-family runs execute 2,100 epochs and 1,644,300 optimizer updates; the five TAPB runs execute 500 epochs and 418,000 updates. Summed per-worker training time is 49.40 hours for the BAN-family roster and 22.41 hours for TAPB. Including the measured validation, scoring and checkpoint-writing intervals gives 51.98 and 24.02 worker-hours, respectively. Individual measured full-fit durations range from 1.95--3.12 hours for BAN-family runs and 4.41--5.07 hours for TAPB.

These elapsed-time sums are measured under shared GPU scheduling, not exclusive GPU-device hours or a controlled speed comparison. They exclude startup, final inference, asset downloading, dictionary preparation and historical search. Shared development encoding of 803 proteins takes 189.69 seconds after loading the frozen ESM model; final-test encoding is separate. The public cost receipt retains all 26 summary hashes and the per-fit counters without duplicating checkpoint aliases.
