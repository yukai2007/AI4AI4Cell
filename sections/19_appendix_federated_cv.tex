Three compound folds plus a baseline repeat gave 165 completed fits; repeat predictions were bitwise identical. Aliases and earlier failures remained inventoried. \paragraph{Actual local three-fold training.}
All campaigns used ten compound-hash client shards from partition 442 and exactly the diagnostic's frozen folds. Label-independent, namespace-qualified SHA256 ordering and sorted rank modulo three assign 111 compounds per fold: 156/159/176 conditions and 26/31/29 positives. Each client filters its shard to fold-training rows before executing source. Preprocessing is profile-local observed-value log1p/min--max scaling and missing-zero fill. Baseline protein/drug blocks are separately L2-normalized, concatenated and normalized. Editable \texttt{build\_features(protein, drug)} is stateless and row-invariant, takes read-only inputs, and returns at most 25,000 features under a shared NumPy allowlist.

Each fold uses the fixed zero-initialized $C=1$ distributed L-BFGS-B objective and unpenalized intercept. Clients send loss, gradient and count, not raw arrays, to the optimizer. A trusted worker scores held-out training-fold rows. Only pooled/fold metrics, counts and source reach research context; no development, gate, partner or final arrays enter search. For pooled OOF AP $P$ and minimum-fold AP $M$, retention requires
\[
P(a')-P(a)>10^{-4}\quad\text{and}\quad M(a')-M(a)>10^{-4}.
\]
This minimum-fold criterion does not require every individual fold to improve.

\paragraph{Native Helmsman adaptation.}
Helmsman executes its upstream planning/coding graph and teams \citep{Li2025Helmsman}, using the common backend/ledger with retrieval disabled. Scripted human input supplies ``yes'' at \texttt{human\_decision} and the fixed task query at \texttt{human\_query}/\texttt{human\_feedback}; no expert guides individual rounds. Exact generated \texttt{task.py} bytes supply the feature function to the immutable CV service. Other wrappers are generated/reviewed but cannot execute. Graph routing, node bodies and stop-at-first-executable-success remain unchanged. Native success is separate from external retention: an executable, unimproved proposal can stop Helmsman while baseline is retained.

\paragraph{Completed costs and failures.}
Direct/loop/Helmsman charged 24/93/123 LLM attempts, with 24/93/122 spawned generation processes and the same numbers completing. Their charged candidate opportunities were 24/24/7, leaving 0/0/17 unused. Across arms, 79 CV evaluations reserved 237 fold slots; 78 completed evaluations produced 234 successful folds. One failed evaluation attempted one fold, giving 235 actual attempts. There were 24 keeps, 30 valid discards and one separate failed evaluation. Loop648's second opportunity violated row invariance; its failure receipt reached the next prompt despite absence from matched measurement history. Ledger-grounded counts therefore record zero unused loop opportunities; history alone undercounts usage.

Helmsman644 failed before process launch: a 146,084-byte prompt exceeded the 131,072-byte single-argument limit, after six completed generations. Its seventh attempt stayed charged; baseline retention remained in both primary analyses. This transport defect is not evidence of inferior research ability. A pre-final declaration permits only proven pre-spawn E2BIG failures in conditional paired sensitivity, not candidate rejection or non-improvement. Excluding paired index644 from loop--Helmsman gives seven-pair AP difference $+0.01855$, 95\% interval $[-0.01073,+0.05140]$, exact $p=0.3281$ and secondary-family Holm $p=0.6563$.

Observed input/output tokens were 5,021,772/654,498, including 3,028,096 cached-input/507,894 reasoning-output tokens as subsets. Successful optimizer receipts contain 75,820 replies and 9,903,232,660/9,908,009,320 request/reply payload bytes. These exclude startup and complete failed-worker traffic and do not measure physical-network savings. Research through final completion took 119.71 minutes. Same-host execution supplies neither differential privacy nor secure aggregation; the historically exposed public final split is not new blind evidence.
