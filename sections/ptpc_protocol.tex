\section*{Appendix C. PTPC observed-response protocol}
\label{app:ptpc-protocol}

\paragraph{Task and source boundary.}
We adapt the publicly available PTPC data in Supplementary Table~8 of
\citet{Sun2026ProteinTalks}. The input is a single compound's \emph{observed}
6-hour and 24-hour protein profiles, together with its molecular fingerprint;
the output is a probability of binary drug efficacy. This follows the PTPC
observed-timepoint setting, not a forecast from untreated cells. Our
ProteinTalks-derived efficacy head is not the full neural-ODE ProteinTalks
model, its pretrained checkpoint, or a reproduction of the authors' few-shot
split. The public subdataset was chosen for input/annotation completeness,
before model comparison. No original-paper score is used as our baseline.

\paragraph{Condition integrity and compound-disjoint roles.}
The adapter checks the complete metadata/matrix row sequence and preserves
the original 5,171 protein features. A condition is a cell line--perturbation
pair with usable unmarked observations at both times and consistent known
efficacy. Controls and marked technical/biological repeats are excluded from
inputs but retained in provenance. Unknown outcomes are excluded, never
converted to negative labels; the adapter explicitly maps Y/N to 1/0.
Forty of 1,019 noncontrol conditions fail the admission rules, leaving 979.
Repeated sample identifiers retain source-row identities rather than being
joined many-to-many.

RDKit 2026.03.5 normalizes molecular representations, selects the largest
organic fragment and uncharges it, then writes canonical isomeric SMILES.
Coordination-metal/inorganic compounds retain their complete cleaned
representation; stereochemistry and isotopes are preserved, and tautomers
are not merged. The admitted data contain 666 standardized compound groups.
One label-independent compound hash assigns all equivalent perturbation IDs,
cell lines and timepoints to a single role, using target fractions
50/10/10/15/15\%. Thus the held-out unit is compound identity, not a row;
scaffold disjointness is not claimed. Initial schema inspection exposed a
few labels before role assignment. The complete chemical closure of exposed
perturbations 101 and 102---ten admitted conditions---is forced into training.
We therefore do not claim zero preflight label access. No class balancing or
outcome-dependent reassignment is performed.

\paragraph{Fixed inputs, preprocessing and fitted models.}
Each condition supplies a $2\times5{,}171$ float32 protein array with NaNs and
an observation mask, and a 2,048-bit radius-2 chiral Morgan fingerprint.
These fingerprints replace, rather than reproduce, ProteinTalks' original
935-dimensional drug representation \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}.
Each observed time profile undergoes log1p and within-profile min--max
normalization. Missing entries are filled by time--protein medians fitted
only on the 491 training conditions; an entirely unobserved training feature
uses zero. Query normalization uses only that query's observed inputs.
Masks determine these operations but are not extra head channels.

The fixed head uses protein and drug Conv1d/ReLU branches of width 32,
duplicates the single-drug vector in its two drug slots, and applies
a $64\!\rightarrow\!32\!\rightarrow\!1$ ReLU classifier
(464,193 parameters). This inherits the phenotype path, not the ODE or
proteome-reconstruction objective \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}.
Every candidate is fitted from scratch with unweighted binary
cross-entropy, AdamW, learning rate $5\times10^{-4}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$,
batch size 64, gradient-norm clipping at 1, and exactly 100 epochs.
The last checkpoint is used: no early stopping, validation-selected epoch,
SWAG, pretrained weights or partner-data fitting. The editable artifact is
the complete head module with a fixed protein/drug-to-logit API and a
two-million-parameter ceiling; the trainer, inputs, preprocessing and scorer
are not editable. All neural arms start from the same fixed-head source.
Separate references are this fixed architecture at each training seed,
cosine 5-nearest-neighbor label averaging, and L2 logistic regression
($C=1$). The latter two use the same train-fitted preprocessing and equally
weighted, separately L2-normalized protein and drug blocks, with no tuning.

\paragraph{Matched research protocol and resource accounting.}
The four arms are direct+pooled, loop+pooled, direct+cards and loop+cards,
each with eight paired training seeds, 142--149: 32 campaigns on one fixed
data split. All receive the same initial source, public research agenda,
evaluation interface and evidence projection for their assigned mode.
The research backend is gpt-5.6-sol with ultra reasoning. Pairing controls
task-training seeds, not identical language-model samples.
Direct generation emits one complete source file per opportunity from the
incumbent and feedback; it has no separate proposal, implementation, repair
or analysis roles. Loop runs the existing production propose/apply/run/finalize
flow (b583291 lineage with the recorded 23f5833 finalizer fix), with an
explicitly installed matched-contract adapter. It retains
hypotheses, source changes, failures, decisions and parent lineage.
It is not an unchanged native-system run: native focus, novelty and
soundness opinions are recorded as advisory, not extra promotion vetoes.
Both arms use the same executable validity checks and selector:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{keep}(c,p)\iff{}&\operatorname{valid}(c)\ \land\
\operatorname{AP}_{\rm dev}(c)>\operatorname{AP}_{\rm dev}(p)+10^{-4}\ \land{}\\
&\operatorname{AP}_{\rm gate}(c)>\operatorname{AP}_{\rm gate}(p)+10^{-4}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The visible gate is selection data, not the final test. AP is sklearn's
non-interpolated average precision, matching the official trainer's metric;
it is not trapezoidal PR area. Undefined one-class AP is explicit, not an
invented score. Rejected or failed candidates retain the last valid parent.

Each campaign has three new-candidate opportunities, at most 24 LLM calls,
four biological fits/evaluations including its baseline, and 20 partner
evaluations (five clients per fitted candidate). Fits use one CPU thread
and a 300-second worker ceiling; partner workers have 60 seconds each.
Scheduled opportunities are reserved at launch, and failures consume their
reserved resources. Only a verified completed fit with the identical
source/data/seed/recipe may be cached within that campaign; another arm
cannot donate a free search fit. Synthetic runtime warm-up is recorded
separately. These are common ceilings, not equal realized calls, tokens or
runtime: in particular, direct uses one generation call per opportunity.
Native prompt composition also differs. The contrast concerns the
research workflow under shared interfaces, not an isolated causal effect
of a hypothesis field or perfectly token-matched reasoning.

The original interactive scheduler exited with signal status 143 after
six dispatch returns; two detached workers subsequently completed normally.
An administrative resume preserved all eight terminal campaigns, checked
their closed ledgers and every frozen runtime/configuration hash, and
dispatched only the 24 untouched campaigns in the original seed blocks and
rotated arm order. The original six return codes remain intact; the two
orphan completions have explicitly unknown process return codes and verified
scientific terminal receipts. No candidate, fit, seed or opportunity was
repeated or added. This operational recovery is recorded separately from
the frozen scientific runtime.

\paragraph{What partners contribute and what learns.}
The partner role is partitioned into five simulated clients by cell line:
A375, HCT116, NCI-H1975, NCI-H2170 and Panc1005, containing respectively
16, 88, 16, 18 and 16 conditions. They are cellular sources within one public
dataset, not independently participating laboratories. Every evaluated
checkpoint is sent to the same five local evaluators in every arm.
A client returns count, Brier score, binary log loss, accuracy, mean
prediction, positive fraction, AP and AUROC, bound to model/data/assignment,
seed and round identities. Cards use six-decimal rounding, at least eight
observations, a 2,048-byte cap and no free text or sample-level arrays.
Pooled mode exposes count-weighted aggregates of the five observation-mean
diagnostics; cards mode additionally exposes the per-client diagnostics.
Client AP/AUROC are explicitly \emph{not poolable}: their average is not
the pooled ranking metric. Public development/gate AP is instead computed
exactly from aligned predictions by the trusted evaluator.

Thus cards versus pooled changes diagnostic resolution, including access
to local ranking diagnostics, on the \emph{same} 154 conditions; it neither
adds tenfold data nor isolates execution topology alone. Direct has the
same local-data interface and can consume cards. The research LLM's weights
never change. Research updates executable model designs and audit state;
the common trainer learns task weights from the authorized training pool;
partners evaluate those weights but do not train or average them. We call
this federated evidence collaboration, not FedAvg. Its biological motivation
is the possibility of contributing local experimental evidence under
measurement-sharing constraints, not an assertion that direct generation
cannot use local data \citep{Sheller2020}.

\paragraph{Execution boundary and audit scope.}
Controllers receive code, scalar scores and evidence packets, not release
arrays. A trusted fit worker preloads train/development/gate data; the
training API uses only training labels. Each partner worker preloads only
its own shard. Dependencies and output handles are initialized before
candidate execution under a process-wide Linux seccomp boundary denying
file opens, network access and new processes. The coordinator checks
identities, row alignment, metrics and durable decisions. Native promotion
must appear in the retained research-branch commit with the matching
decision and selected-source hashes, not merely a database intention.
This supports inspection of what was evaluated and retained, not proof
that a textual hypothesis caused an improvement. The coordinator and local
services are trusted; in-memory label introspection, tensor-library exploits,
denial of service and information encoded in weights/diagnostics are not
ruled out. No differential privacy, secure aggregation or real-institution
deployment is claimed.

A separate engineering check replays one allowlisted baseline checkpoint
centrally, with the identical five partner shards, stored preprocessor and
per-client inference. All 35 rounded diagnostic fields and five card hashes
match the local-worker receipts. This is fixed-input/model execution
equivalence, not a final efficacy result or federation advantage; it does
not reseal the historical campaign or benchmark communication latency.

\paragraph{Final access, deployment and statistical interpretation.}
The frozen protocol requires all 32 campaigns to reach terminal states,
then seals every selected source, checkpoint, receipt and budget.
An unresolved reservation, invalid baseline or integrity failure blocks
final evaluation; failed candidates remain in the campaign summaries.
A second deployment seal explicitly aliases the selected last-epoch
checkpoints because search and deployment have identical train/seed/recipe
contracts. No additional neural refit or partner-data training is implied.
The same-seed fixed-head references are identified baseline aliases;
cosine/logistic are fitted once on training data, not presented as eight
independent replicates. Only after both seals are verified is the final
loader enabled. Final candidate workers receive label-free features;
the trusted parent scores aligned predictions. A single recorded dispatch
prevents score-dependent reruns or post-test candidate replacement.

The specified primary endpoint is final AP. The two primary paired
contrasts are loop+cards minus direct+cards, and loop+cards minus loop+pooled.
We report all eight seed values, means/sample SDs, paired differences,
and descriptive 95\% paired campaign-bootstrap intervals (10,000 draws).
Two-sided exact sign-flip tests enumerate $2^8$ assignments, with Holm
correction across the two contrasts at $\alpha=0.05$; the predeclared
practical AP difference is 0.02. Sign-flip inference assumes exchangeable,
sign-symmetric paired differences. Intervals describe campaign variation
conditional on this fixed compound split, not uncertainty across future
laboratories, cell lines or chemical populations. AUROC, accuracy at 0.5
and log loss are secondary. No seeds, splits or endpoints are changed in
response to final outcomes. All 32 campaigns and 42 final method/seed
records completed. The historical results in Appendix N.2.3 do not support either
predeclared superiority claim; the engineering checks are reported
separately from that outcome.
