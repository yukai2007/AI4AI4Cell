\section*{Appendix I. Cell-task inputs and exact scoring}

\paragraph{Shared Norman archive and distinct objectives.}
The two Norman tasks use released scDEBART predictions and condition-level,
scVI-derived response estimates \citep{sung2026scdebart} from K562
single/combinatorial CRISPRa experiments \citep{norman2019genetic}.
These are archived endpoints, not new observations or longitudinal data. The three source
archives contain 124/124/129 conditions. Removing all 81 directly targeted
genes leaves 2,998 downstream expression coordinates. Thirty known
prediction--response pairs provide calibration; the query-label roles
contain 46 development, 51 visible-gate and 51 loop-excluded diagnostic
conditions. These roles overlap in biological context and component genes;
the last archive was inspected during environment construction.

\paragraph{Reverse identification.}
The policy receives a query's downstream response and a library of frozen
predicted responses, and ranks the eligible interventions. The exact source
is present once; the 30 calibration interventions are excluded, leaving
94/94/99 candidates across the three roles. For $Q$ queries, the primary
score is $Q^{-1}\sum_q\mathbf{1}[\widehat a_q=a_q^\star]$.
An exact two-gene intervention is one label: recovering just one component
does not receive partial primary credit. Table N.1 uses the 51-query diagnostic
role. This is closed-library identification, not open-set causal discovery
or a reproduction of scDEBART's differently constructed reverse benchmark.

\paragraph{Sequential phenocopy search.}
Here the source intervention is removed as well, leaving 93/93/98
alternatives. A deterministic policy chooses three distinct actions;
each query reveals that action's archived response for the next choice.
The initial features are the desired response, frozen candidate predictions
and the same 30 historical pairs. Let $z_q$ be the desired downstream
response and $y_{qa_{qj}}$ the revealed response of action $a_{qj}$.
The reported metric is
\[
\operatorname{AUC3}=\frac{1}{3Q}\sum_{q=1}^{Q}\sum_{t=1}^{3}
 \max_{j\leq t}\operatorname{cos}(z_q,y_{qa_{qj}}).
\]
It averages best-so-far observed similarity over the three steps, then over
queries; it is not ROC-AUC, classification accuracy or an early-stopping
policy. The locked cosine reference orders candidates by predicted
similarity before observing their responses. Alternatives may share a
component gene with the source, so success is not necessarily a new
mechanism. The retained policies' zero feedback-action-change diagnostic
limits any claim of within-episode adaptation.

\paragraph{CEX cold-cell virtual screening.}
This custom cold-cell/known-drug replay uses GDSC1-derived measurements
\citep{yang2013gdsc} and frozen out-of-fold PGBridge/chemical predictions. It has
389 unique cell-context identifiers (the upstream field is named
\texttt{PatientID}), 149 structure representatives and 48,376 measured
context--action rows; each episode offers 16--149 actions. The
calibration/development/visible-gate/diagnostic roles contain 78/117/97/97
contexts. Calibration contains 9,573 already-observed outcomes. Every
prediction contributing to an out-of-fold mean held that context out of
its own outer training split. The intermediate predictions and split
manifests are required dependencies, not reconstructed by the loop.
Inherited provenance omits the exact GDSC release; the resource citation
does not establish this custom split or scorer.

Write $r_{qa}$ for measured drug-response AUC (lower means greater
sensitivity), and $\tau_{qa}=\min(0.70,\overline{q25}^{\,\mathrm{train}}_{qa})$
for the fixed pair-specific threshold. The latter averages the appropriate
outer models' training-only 25th-percentile thresholds. For the three
distinct selected actions,
\[
\operatorname{AURB3}=\frac{1}{3Q}\sum_{q=1}^{Q}\sum_{t=1}^{3}
 \max_{j\leq t}\bigl(\tau_{qa_{qj}}-r_{qa_{qj}}\bigr).
\]
Margins remain signed, including negative values; they are not clipped to
zero. The reference selects ascending frozen raw-PG predicted AUC, not
cosine similarity. A separate absolute-response diagnostic averages
$1-\min_{j\leq t}r_{qa_{qj}}$. Relative-threshold improvement need not improve
this absolute objective, nor establish efficacy, safety or clinical benefit.

Sequential bootstrap intervals compare fixed policies over queries, not
independent campaigns. All Cell results are retrospective; DTI and PTPC
use their separately stated campaign-level analyses.
