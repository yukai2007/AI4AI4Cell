\paragraph{Shared development preparation.}
A separately declared manual probe evaluates six parameter-free combiners on the 5,604 saved validation predictions: uniform cosine weights 0.1, 0.2 and 0.3; uncertainty-dependent weights $0.3e^{-8|p_{\rm BAN}-0.5|}$ and $0.5e^{-8|p_{\rm BAN}-0.5|}$; and weight 0.3 only when $|p_{\rm BAN}-0.5|<0.2$. Predictions are $(1-w)p_{\rm BAN}+wp_{\rm cosine}$. The resulting common starting source is $0.7p_{\rm BAN}+0.3p_{\rm cosine}$. All six manual candidates and their development results are retained; their preparation is not attributed to an autonomous harness.

The validation predictions are split by a label-independent canonical-compound hash into halves of 2,884 and 2,720 rows with no compound overlap. Both halves already participated in DrugBAN checkpoint selection, so they are visible reused development rather than a fresh holdout. Direct proposals independently receive the starting source and its aggregate evidence. The production loop invokes its actual proposal, implementation, measurement, retention and persistent-history stages, and receives the current incumbent and previous outcomes.

\paragraph{Common contract.}
Each trajectory has three candidate opportunities, at most 24 LLM calls and four scoring opportunities including the baseline. There are zero new task-weight fits. Candidates must return finite probabilities in $[0,1]$ through a stateless, row-invariant interface, without modifying inputs, reading labels or using file/network access. Synthetic and real applications check row permutation, repetition, added-row and singleton consistency. Source is bounded to 24 KB and a numerical API allowlist. Failed generations and invalid implementations consume the declared opportunities. Retention requires both visible-half AUROCs to improve by more than $10^{-4}$; AP and BCE are secondary diagnostics.

The research model receives no per-row predictions, labels or test scores. All ten selected sources and ledgers must be sealed before any selected program is applied to the saved random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein test vectors. Final reporting retains 30 campaign/endpoint records and nine reference/endpoint records: fixed starting combiner, DrugBAN and cosine. The sole primary contrast is loop minus direct unseen-drug AUROC, paired over the five trajectories, with a two-sided exact sign-flip test over 32 assignments and a descriptive 10,000-draw paired bootstrap. These comparisons condition on one pair of base predictors and previously exposed public tests. A better combiner is distinct from a loop-over-direct gain, and neither alone establishes state of the art.

\subsection*{M.1 Completed outcomes}
Table~\ref{tab:dti-program-search} reports both controllers and all three fixed references.

All ten campaigns and all 39 final records complete; saved predictions reproduce the original scorer exactly. The loop's mean AUROC increases over DrugBAN by 0.0137/0.0167/0.0364 on random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein tests. Much of this gain is already achieved by the common manual combiner. Further source search improves modestly over that starting rule for both controllers, but direct generation has the higher mean AUROC on all three tests. The primary loop-minus-direct difference is $-0.000385$, with descriptive 95\% paired-bootstrap interval $[-0.001055,+0.000568]$ and exact two-sided $p=0.5625$. This is a successful predictive combination, not evidence that the loop outperforms direct generation.

Direct uses 15 LLM calls, 238,278 input tokens and 28,627 output tokens; the loop uses 61 calls, 1,180,437 input tokens and 43,747 output tokens. Cached input tokens are 193,408 and 742,912, respectively, and are included in those input totals. Both arms consume 15 candidate opportunities and 20 scoring opportunities, with zero failed candidates and zero new weight fits. Direct retains seven changes; the loop retains five. All 15 native candidate decisions have matching persistent records. Thus the narrow two-predictor interface gives direct generation a strong, lower-call reference; the loop's extra stages do not earn a measured mean ranking advantage here.

\subsection*{M.2 Frozen-program transfer to TAPB}
The retained probability programs also improve a stronger TAPB reference without changing their source or repeating research. Before the transfer evaluator opens TAPB test predictions, a separate manifest fixes the one-to-one pairing of research indices 542--546 with TAPB fit seeds 342--346 for both controllers. Each original function receives the corresponding TAPB probability as its first positional argument, replacing DrugBAN; its second input remains the same full-training cosine 5-NN probability. Source bytes, parameter names and internal numerical operations are unchanged. The original $0.7p_{\rm TAPB}+0.3p_{\rm cosine}$ manual mixture is applied to every fit. No program is reselected, tuned, clipped by the adapter, retrained or replaced on failure.

This evaluation reports 30 transferred-program, 15 manual-mixture and 18 existing-reference labels across all three test splits. All 45 program applications succeed. There are five existing TAPB fits, not ten independently trained models; means and sample SDs describe the five fixed research/fit pairings. For index 542, the retained loop source is the unchanged manual rule, producing an exact alias on each split; these three aliases remain reported, giving 63 labels and 60 distinct prediction-artifact hashes. The transfer takes 4.57 seconds of application orchestration, with zero additional LLM calls, searches or task-weight fits; earlier research and TAPB training costs are not erased by this reuse.

The transferred loop raises mean AUROC from TAPB's 0.9545/0.9328/0.8868 to 0.9669/0.9512/0.9149 on random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein tests (Table~\ref{tab:dti-tapb-program-transfer}). Unseen-drug AP rises from 0.9416 to 0.9607 and log loss decreases from 0.4342 to 0.2956. The shared manual mixture already reaches AUROC 0.9666/0.9507/0.9143, while transferred direct programs reach 0.9672/0.9515/0.9158. Thus the main transferable benefit is predictor complementarity; retained source refinements add small gains over the manual start, but the loop does not exceed direct generation on mean ranking performance.

The two predeclared primary contrasts use unseen-drug AUROC, exact two-sided sign-flips over 32 assignments, 10,000 paired-bootstrap draws and Holm correction over both contrasts. Loop minus TAPB is $+0.018338$, positive in all five pairs, with descriptive 95\% interval $[+0.014068,+0.022934]$, exact $p=0.0625$ and Holm-adjusted $p=0.125$. Loop minus direct is $-0.000371$, interval $[-0.000862,+0.000240]$, and exact/adjusted $p=0.375$. These five-pair descriptive intervals do not replace the corrected tests. All five metrics, all pairs and both contrasts are included in the aggregate companion files.

The original tests and earlier program-study outcomes were already publicly exposed, and some fixed-reference TAPB predictions had been generated by its existing watcher when this transfer was declared. Their scores were not used to choose transferred programs, pairings or tuning. The transfer manifest was frozen before this evaluator accessed those predictions, and execution waited for all five 100-epoch fits and all 15 TAPB endpoints to close. This is transfer of previously selected DrugBAN-based programs, not new harness research on TAPB or a new blind benchmark. Original-row alignment, the unchanged scorer at threshold 0.5, source/checkpoint hashes and all 63 reported labels pass independent post-completion verification.

\subsection*{M.3 Post-final explanation of predictor complementarity}
We decompose the already saved fixed $0.7p_{\rm TAPB}+0.3p_{\rm cosine}$ outputs for all five TAPB fit seeds and all three tests, without new prediction, fitting, tuning or hypothesis tests. For a positive--negative pair, define credit $c=\mathbf{1}[p_+>p_-]+\tfrac12\mathbf{1}[p_+=p_-]$. With $N=N_+N_-$ pairs, repaired and broken credit are
\[
R=\frac{\sum\max(c_{\rm manual}-c_{\rm TAPB},0)}{N},\qquad
B=\frac{\sum\max(c_{\rm TAPB}-c_{\rm manual},0)}{N}.
\]
Hence $R-B=\mathrm{AUROC}_{\rm manual}-\mathrm{AUROC}_{\rm TAPB}$. Integer half-credit identities hold exactly for all 15 records; the maximum discrepancy from the unchanged floating-point scorer is $2.22\times10^{-16}$.

Cosine is weaker in overall AUROC, yet gives complementary ordering credit on TAPB's strict errors. Conditional denominators summed across seeds are 1,722,057/1,186,577/889,588 pair occurrences on random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein tests.
