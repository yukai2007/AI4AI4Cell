The gate gain over the mean single-client replay is 4.31 percentage points; the aggregate ties the strongest single client rather than beating every client. Aggregated and centralized variance differ by at most 6.66\(\times\)10\textsuperscript{-16}, and scores agree exactly. Binary statistic payloads total 479,760 bytes per fold, excluding transport/security overhead. This is a post-search fixed-algorithm information replay, not fifteen additional federated campaigns or a measured tenfold change in real laboratories' willingness to participate.

The cell audit contains both a useful implementation and a refuted hypothesis. A retained reverse-identification policy uses shrunk residual variances in a Mahalanobis distance. A visible 2\(\times\)2 replay shows no gain from an unweighted Euclidean substitution alone, while reliability weighting helps both cosine and amplitude-sensitive distances. In a separate federated engineering run, client cards motivate conditional residual transfer; its executed development score falls and the native system rolls it back. The latter demonstrates consumption and refutation of evidence, not a positive treatment effect. Sequential/CEX feedback-counterfactual action changes are zero for the retained non-federated policies, so those policies do not demonstrate within-episode feedback adaptation.

Audit coverage: 15/15 formal search campaigns have terminal receipts. Only visible development/selection feedback is used; no final-test predictions or labels are read. Engineering canaries are excluded.

Retained case (our native harness, seed 42, item 3; source `49d2a9be7d10`). Recorded rationale: Per-example, per-head RMS normalization produced the strongest retained gain (+0.02804 development AUROC), but unconditional summation still assumes both normalized heads are equally useful; learning two global softmax gates, initialized uniformly and multiplied by two to preserve the original sum at initialization, should suppress redundant head signals and improve development\_auroc. Intervention: ban.py head aggregation: unconditional head sum -> learned scale-preserving simplex-weighted head sum. Development AUROC changed from 0.8887 to 0.8905; visible-selection AUROC changed from 0.8904 to 0.8975. The respective deltas were +0.001858 and +0.007132. The actual native decision was `keep`. It remains the terminal selected source. Finalization replayed pre-interruption responses externally; unavailable transient trajectory fields were left unreconstructed.

Rejected case (our native harness, seed 42, item 2; source `62016ac3a51b`). Recorded rationale: Starting from RMS-normalized attention maps, adding a parameter-light residual interaction formed by the elementwise product of attention-marginal-pooled drug and protein projections will preserve complementary first-order agreement signals and increase development\_auroc. Intervention: normalized bilinear interaction alone -> normalized bilinear interaction plus attention-conditioned Hadamard residual. Development AUROC changed from 0.8887 to 0.8970; visible-selection AUROC changed from 0.8904 to 0.8885. The respective deltas were +0.008347 and -0.001903. The actual native decision was `discard`. The candidate was not promoted.

Matched source hashes, attested evaluations and native trajectories verify both decisions. Persisted follow-up memory is agent-generated interpretation, not an independently established mechanism. Cases follow fixed method/seed/item order, not effect-size ranking. Full hypotheses, feedback, stage counts, costs and recovery receipts remain in the machine-readable audit. External recovery is not evidence of native automatic fault tolerance.
