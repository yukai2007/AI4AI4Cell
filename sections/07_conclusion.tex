\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop improves predictors through collaborative training and executable design search. Laboratory participation improves all five endpoints, and the complete system exceeds single-laboratory references on four. With ten laboratories fixed, design search adds 1.96 DTI AUROC and 6.77 Norman Top-1 points. Independently, trajectory-guided allocation adds 10.84 Norman Top-1 points at matched budgets. Source-defined DTI and proteomics studies demonstrate beneficial cross-collection transfer.
