\section*{7. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Bio couples client-local parameter fitting with a distributed proposal--evaluation loop, so that biological measurements remaining at their source can support both shared predictors and executable design revision. Across drug--target interaction prediction, observed-response proteomics and cell perturbation identification, a three-configuration comparison tests the access-enabled system, while a six-arm ablation separates laboratory participation from research feedback. Completed retrospective tests show held-out gains from additional laboratories; matched-access feedback is not yet a consistent independent advantage, and locally selected revisions do not automatically generalize. The common framework offers a practical path toward self-improving biological AI under data-sharing constraints, while making the remaining evaluation-and-transfer problem explicit.
