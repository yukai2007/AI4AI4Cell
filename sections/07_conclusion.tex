\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop turns laboratory-local training and evaluation into two shared resources: aggregated model updates for collaborative fitting and aggregated development evidence for deciding what to propose and where to spend training. Collaborative access is the dominant effect across the studied endpoints, with the largest gains in drug--target and proteomic prediction, while the cell identification tasks remain a low-headroom stress test near chance. Design search and evidence-guided allocation each improve the endpoint whose candidate set has measurable headroom, and independent sites, larger libraries and a random-sweep control are the natural next steps (Appendix~A.6).
