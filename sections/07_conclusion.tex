\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop connects collaborative fitting to the evaluation and revision of executable biological model designs. The shared interface supports three task families, beneficial transfer from compatible studies, and improved double-gene perturbation identification through selective longer fitting. Matched-access searches yield above-random model variants, with task-dependent history effects and competitive conventional controls. Sealed split-panel confirmation identifies development selection as the main limit; biological-split robustness and compute-matched access comparisons remain priorities. These controlled results provide a practical experimental basis for collaborative biological model development.
