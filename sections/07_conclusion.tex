\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop extends collaborative learning from parameter fitting to iterative model development. Laboratories contribute model updates for shared fitting and development evidence for research decisions through a common execution interface. Across the evaluated biological tasks, BioCoLoop improves most primary endpoints. Controlled studies show that development history changes proposal trajectories and can reach useful configurations earlier, while a separate matched-budget study demonstrates improved training allocation. Collaborative participation and evidence-guided decisions provide complementary components of a research loop in which laboratories jointly inform both how a model is fitted and what is tested next.
