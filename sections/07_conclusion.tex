\section*{7. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Cell couples client-local parameter fitting with an evidence-guided research harness, enabling biological measurements held by multiple laboratories to improve both a shared predictor and its executable design. Across DTI, observed-response proteomics and three cell-perturbation strata, the complete system leads four of five main-table endpoints. The factorial evaluation attributes the broadest gains to multi-laboratory participation, while the compute-matched racing experiment shows that persistent feedback can also improve how a fixed training budget is allocated: four held-out wins, eight ties and no losses, with a +10.84-point mean gain on Norman. A shared proposal schema, evidence-card history and laboratory interface make this process reproducible across task families and provide a practical foundation for collaborative, self-improving biological AI.
