\section*{6. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop connects laboratory-local parameter fitting to an evidence-guided research loop through shared proposal, training and evaluation interfaces. The complete system leads four of five main-comparison endpoints, and compute-matched allocation improves Norman Top-1 by 10.84 points on average. Independent-source experiments demonstrate transfer gains from selected DTI and cellular collections, while laboratory-count and dual-proposer studies identify the effects of partitioning, source compatibility and research budget. The results distinguish parameter-learning gains, efficient allocation and proposal-history effects, providing an empirical basis for collaborative biological model development and more effective research feedback.
