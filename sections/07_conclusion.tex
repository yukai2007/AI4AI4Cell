\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop connects laboratory-local parameter fitting to an evidence-guided research loop through shared proposal, training and evaluation interfaces. The complete system leads four of five main-comparison endpoints, and compute-matched allocation improves Norman Top-1 by 10.84 points on average. Source-defined laboratory studies show transfer gains in DTI and proteomics, while cell comparisons highlight the importance of source compatibility. Laboratory-count and dual-proposer studies reveal how partitioning and research budget shape learning. The results distinguish parameter-learning gains, efficient allocation and proposal-history effects, providing an empirical basis for collaborative biological model development and more effective research feedback.
