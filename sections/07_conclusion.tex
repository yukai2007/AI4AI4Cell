\section*{7. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Cell connects executable-design research to client-local biological training and evaluation. A fixed research LLM changes source, local fitters learn task weights, and measured outcomes guide the next revision. Improved designs reach DTI AUROC 0.9710/0.9588/0.9317 and proteomics AP 0.3727; access to ten rather than one simulated client improves a fixed design's AP by 6.11 points. Matched strong-start controls distinguish these practical gains from the smaller, variable effect of loop organization. The framework enables local measurements to contribute to both shared model designs and their fitted parameters.
