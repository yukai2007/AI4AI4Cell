\section*{7. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Bio connects distributed biological measurements to shared predictive parameters and evidence-guided computational designs. A common six-arm protocol spans DTI, proteomics and three cell-perturbation strata, separately testing laboratory participation, design search and research feedback. Completed first-seed development results show participation gains, while loop and matched-budget direct search tie on the available primary endpoints. The unfinished three-seed and final-test results remain N/A. This version establishes a consistent implementation and an explicit attribution framework; the ongoing evaluation must complete before broader performance claims are made.
