\section*{7. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Bio connects distributed biological measurements to shared predictive parameters and evidence-guided computational designs. A three-configuration comparison tests the access-enabled system, while a six-arm ablation separates laboratory participation from research feedback. Completed retrospective tests show gains in proteomic efficacy and single-gene identification; unfinished evaluations stay N/A. The common framework provides a concrete basis for collaborative biological research without centralizing raw training responses.
