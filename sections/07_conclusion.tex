\section*{7. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Cell makes biological algorithm research inspectable through executable artifacts, measured decisions and verifiable evidence transfer. The completed studies show that these engineering properties and additional diagnostic access do not guarantee held-out gains: DTI improvements are uncertain, and the matched proteomics study does not support the intended harness or client-card advantage. The useful output is a concrete local-evidence research interface and a complete record of its measured limits. Establishing stronger predictive or cross-institutional benefits requires new independent evidence, not reinterpreting audit completeness as performance superiority.
