\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop combines collaborative training with executable design search and exceeds single-laboratory references on four of five endpoints. With ten laboratories fixed, design search adds 1.96 DTI AUROC and 6.77 Norman Top-1 points. Independently, trajectory-guided allocation adds 10.84 Norman Top-1 points at matched budgets; source-defined DTI and proteomics studies demonstrate beneficial cross-collection transfer.
