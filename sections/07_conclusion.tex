\section*{6. Conclusion}
AI4AI4Cell connects laboratory-local parameter fitting to an evidence-guided research loop. The same proposal, training and evaluation interfaces support DTI, observed-response proteomics and cell perturbation. The complete system leads four of five endpoints in the main comparison, and the compute-matched racing experiment improves allocation of a fixed training budget, including a 10.84-point mean Top-1 gain on Norman. These results establish a practical way for distributed biological measurements to inform both predictive parameters and computational research decisions.
