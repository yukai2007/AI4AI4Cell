\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop makes biological model development collaborative without pooling raw data. Compared with a laboratory working alone on common held-out tests, ten-laboratory development improves all five primary endpoints, beyond single-laboratory conventional search; distinct studies improve target prediction, and evidence-guided allocation improves perturbation identification at a matched budget. Collaboration is simulated on shared compute; stabilizing development selection and real multi-site deployment are natural next steps.
