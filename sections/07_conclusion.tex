\section*{5. Conclusion}
BioCoLoop improves existing biological predictors through collaborative training and executable design search. Under the same research policy, increasing laboratory participation improves all five endpoints; the complete system exceeds single-laboratory references on four. With ten laboratories fixed, design search adds 1.96 DTI AUROC points and 6.77 Norman Top-1 points over the fixed recipe. Independently, trajectory-guided allocation adds 10.84 Norman Top-1 points at matched training and evaluation budgets. Source-defined DTI and proteomics studies also demonstrate beneficial cross-collection transfer.
