\subsection*{C.1 Shared biological task interface}
AI-Scientist-v2 \citep{Yamada2025AIScientistV2} and AI-Researcher \citep{Tang2025AIResearcher} use BioCoLoop's fitting and evaluation service, at source versions \texttt{96bd51617cfd} and \texttt{f9a6f8480860}, respectively \citep{AIScientistV2Code,AIResearcherCode}. Both use the registered executable design space of twelve configurations, at most six proposals, 100 rounds per valid candidate and the shared held-out scorer. The service fits D00 afresh, then executes submitted \texttt{design\_id} values; the hypothesis, experiment and expected-effect fields record rationale. Repeated or invalid requests consume slots, and agents may stop early. The original development score/loss rule selects checkpoints.

AI-Scientist-v2 retains its experiment manager, tree-search paths, reviews and journal. AI-Researcher retains native idea selection, planning, implementation, review handoffs and iterative analysis. Prompts use the registered design interface; arbitrary generated-code execution, external retrieval, figure review and manuscript generation are disabled. The matched blocks use local Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (temperature 0.7, top-$p$ 0.9, at most 512 generated tokens per call) and GPT-5.6 Luna (low reasoning effort, text-only bridge, tools disabled). Adapters allow at most 160 model calls and 28,000 input tokens per call, raised to 65,536 for the four starred Luna AI-Researcher retries (C.2). Qwen uses generation seeds; Luna labels identify training/search repetitions. Per-run call counts, token usage and transport receipts document the variable language-model costs.

\subsection*{C.2 Prediction quality and execution outcomes}
A scored run completes its agent's research process within the registered cap, seals development selection and passes independent held-out rescoring. Table~\ref{tab:public-harness-comparison-three-seed} summarizes seeds 42--44; fixed, direct and BioCoLoop rows are complete. Daggers mark partial cells and dashes mark unscored cells, without imputation. Partial cells are Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct AI-Researcher on VCC ($2/3$ seeds, $F_{\mathrm{tool}}$) and GPT-5.6 Luna AI-Scientist-v2 on Tahoe ($2/3$, $F_{\mathrm{svc}}$).

Four Luna AI-Researcher cells exceeded the 28,000-token admission ($F_{\mathrm{ctx}}$) and were re-run at 65,536 tokens with the same tasks, seeds, design space, six-proposal cap, training and scorer. Their completed cells carry stars, with per-seed records retained. BindingDB (DTI), the longest native refinement trajectory, remains unscored ($0/3$): one frozen seed reached 29,167 input tokens ($F_{\mathrm{ctx}}$), and two terminated in service transport ($F_{\mathrm{svc}}$). Extended-admission retries also ended in transport before held-out selection was sealed. The final table column gives mean rank across completed endpoints.

Receipts distinguish native pipeline ($F_{\mathrm{pipe}}$), tool-transport ($F_{\mathrm{tool}}$), registered context-limit ($F_{\mathrm{ctx}}$) and Luna service-transport ($F_{\mathrm{svc}}$) terminations before sealed selection. The seed-42 Qwen AI-Scientist-v2 DTI example trains three candidates before substage schema validation fails, leaving its incumbent unsealed and unscored.

Held-out scoring follows sealed development selection. An independent verifier recomputes each scored cell's metric from saved predictions and checks checkpoint and artifact hashes. DTI discrepancies are at most $10^{-15}$. Records retain raw calls, native stage history, candidate requests, selections and verification receipts.

\subsection*{C.3 Task-transport repair}
The AI-Researcher adaptation removes repeated task packets from stage prompts, preserving the complete task and evidence in system prompts, native reasoning and tool results. The bridge accepts an unambiguous \texttt{parameters} alias for \texttt{arguments} and standard single-tool-call envelopes without inferring actions or values. This revision preserves model weights, context allowance, training and scoring. It applies across all five endpoints and the Luna sensitivity; failures remain recorded.
