\section*{2. Related work and comparison scope}
AIDE searches candidate programs through drafting, debugging and improvement conditioned on summarized evaluations \citep{Jiang2025AIDE}. The original AI Scientist connects ideas to executed experiments, notes, replanning and writing \citep{Lu2024AIScientist}. We extend this research-loop setting to executable designs trained and evaluated through local biological workers.

DrugEvolve combines research, implementation, debugging, evaluation, analysis, memory and algorithm lineage across 11 drug-development task families and 120 test sets \citep{Zhou2026DrugEvolve,DrugEvolveCode}. Our DTI comparison uses its released Creation pipeline and local database, with explicit model-transport and evaluator adaptations. The released DrugBAN-Evo module is a fixed algorithm reference, distinct from rerunning that controller.

DrugBAN combines molecular graphs, protein sequences and bilinear attention \citep{Bai2023DrugBAN,DrugBANCode}. We reconstruct the pinned encoder--interaction--classifier model and restrict architecture search to its BAN component. All selected architectures transfer to the same full-data recipe. TAPB supplies a contemporary pretrained-protein reference on the identical split \citep{lin2025tapb}, testing predictive strength separately from workflow comparisons.

Simple linear models can match or exceed deep models on gene-perturbation response benchmarks \citep{ahlmann2025linear}. Those forward-expression tasks differ from our policy endpoints, but motivate strong fixed starting models and non-neural references.

Biological FL already supports cell-type classification in scFed \citep{Wang2024scFed} and batch correction through local VAE fitting and latent shifts in FedscGen \citep{Bakhtiari2025FedscGen}. Tabula/Chiron combines single-cell foundation modeling with agent-enabled federated training \citep{Ding2026Tabula}; its released platform exposes training and monitoring to agents and coordinates local trainers through Flower FedAvg \citep{ChironCode}. Our focus is the hypothesis--source--measurement--revision cycle and controlled attribution of its predictive gains, rather than agent access to biological FL alone.

Federated model development also includes FedEx, which tunes client hyperparameters while training shared weights \citep{Khodak2021FedEx}, and Helmsman, which combines human-verified planning, modular FL code generation and simulation-driven refinement \citep{Li2025Helmsman}. Native Helmsman and direct generation provide controller comparisons with the same local-training access. Federated averaging \citep{McMahan2017} concerns inner weight training; our proteomics adapter instead aggregates local objectives and gradients, while the outer loop revises source with a fixed research LLM.
