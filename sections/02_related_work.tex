\section*{2. Related work and comparison scope}
AIDE searches candidate programs through drafting, debugging and improvement conditioned on summarized evaluations \citep{Jiang2025AIDE}. The original AI Scientist connects ideas to executed experiments, notes, replanning and writing \citep{Lu2024AIScientist}. We extend this research-loop setting to executable designs trained and evaluated through local biological workers.

DrugEvolve combines research, implementation, debugging, evaluation, analysis, memory and algorithm lineage across 11 drug-development task families and 120 test sets \citep{Zhou2026DrugEvolve,DrugEvolveCode}. Our earlier DTI controller comparison used its released Creation pipeline and local database, with explicit model-transport and evaluator adaptations (Appendix L). The current factorial study instead uses the common Qwen controller and TAPB adapter. The released DrugBAN-Evo module is a fixed algorithm reference, distinct from rerunning that controller.

DrugBAN combines molecular graphs, protein sequences and bilinear attention \citep{Bai2023DrugBAN,DrugBANCode}. It provides a native-model reference and the starting architecture for the earlier source-search study. Our recent-family DTI comparison uses TAPB with public frozen protein features and fresh task-trained parameters \citep{lin2025tapb}. ProteinTalks supplies the architecture for an observed-response efficacy-head adaptation \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}; scDEBART supplies public-pretrained cell representations \citep{sung2026scdebart}. Matching the task and initialization is essential: published model-family performance does not transfer automatically to our client-restricted benchmarks.

Simple linear models can match or exceed deep models on gene-perturbation response benchmarks \citep{ahlmann2025linear}. Those forward-expression tasks differ from our policy endpoints, but motivate strong fixed starting models and non-neural references.

Biological FL already supports cell-type classification in scFed \citep{Wang2024scFed} and batch correction through local VAE fitting and latent shifts in FedscGen \citep{Bakhtiari2025FedscGen}. Tabula/Chiron combines single-cell foundation modeling with agent-enabled federated training \citep{Ding2026Tabula}; its released platform exposes training and monitoring to agents and coordinates local trainers through Flower FedAvg \citep{ChironCode}. Our focus is the hypothesis--source--measurement--revision cycle and controlled attribution of its predictive gains, rather than agent access to biological FL alone.

Federated model development also includes FedEx, which tunes client hyperparameters while training shared weights \citep{Khodak2021FedEx}, and Helmsman, which combines human-verified planning, modular FL code generation and simulation-driven refinement \citep{Li2025Helmsman}. Native Helmsman and direct generation are included in our earlier proteomics source-search study. The current common implementation uses federated parameter averaging \citep{McMahan2017} in all three task families; the earlier proteomics local-gradient optimizer is a separate protocol. Our specific focus is using distributed evaluation evidence for iterative design research and measuring that effect separately from access to more local training data.
