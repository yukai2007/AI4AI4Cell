\section*{2. Related work and comparison scope}
Automated research systems connect computational proposals to measured outcomes. AIDE searches candidate programs through drafting, debugging and improvement conditioned on summarized evaluations \citep{Jiang2025AIDE}. The original AI Scientist connects ideas to executed experiments, notes, replanning and writing \citep{Lu2024AIScientist}. DrugEvolve combines research, implementation, debugging, evaluation, analysis, memory and algorithm lineage across drug-development tasks \citep{Zhou2026DrugEvolve,DrugEvolveCode}. These systems motivate persistent experiment memory and executable revision. AI4AI4Cell extends that research-loop abstraction to measurements distributed across data-owning laboratories: proposal selection is driven by aggregate client evidence, and the same evidence interface is evaluated across DTI, proteomics and cell perturbation.

Collaborative biological training supplies the complementary parameter-learning interface. Medical multi-institutional studies and biological applications such as scFed, FedscGen and Tabula/Chiron use local fitting and parameter aggregation for cell-type classification, batch correction or foundation-model training \citep{Sheller2020,Wang2024scFed,Bakhtiari2025FedscGen,Ding2026Tabula,ChironCode}. AI4AI4Cell uses sample-weighted parameter aggregation \citep{McMahan2017} as a shared inner training pipeline, then exposes aggregate development evidence to an outer harness that can revise the executable design itself.

Automated design can also interact with local-update training: FedEx tunes client hyperparameters while training shared weights \citep{Khodak2021FedEx}, and Helmsman generates and repairs collaborative-learning systems through planning, modular coding and simulation-driven refinement \citep{Li2025Helmsman}. AI4AI4Cell focuses this combination on biological data holders and contributes a common proposal--measurement--revision interface: the harness records the hypothesis, executable intervention, aggregate outcome and retention decision, then supplies that structured history to the next proposal.

DrugBAN combines molecular graphs, protein sequences and bilinear attention \citep{Bai2023DrugBAN,DrugBANCode}; our DTI study uses the more recent TAPB architecture with public frozen protein features and freshly task-trained parameters \citep{lin2025tapb}. ProteinTalks supplies the architecture for the observed-response efficacy head \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}, and scDEBART supplies public-pretrained cell representations \citep{sung2026scdebart}. Simple linear models can match or exceed deep models on gene-perturbation response benchmarks \citep{ahlmann2025linear}, motivating strong fixed recipes and simple references alongside the shared research interface.
