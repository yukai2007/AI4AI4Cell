\paragraph{Version and scope.}
The current main protocol uses \texttt{core\_v3.py}, \texttt{run\_v3.py} and the common \texttt{research\_v2.py} candidate generator. Version 3 refers to the orchestration, feedback and state-buffer correction, not a new foundation model. Historical V1/V2 outputs and failed fits are preserved. All V3 scientific fits start afresh; earlier task-trained weights are not reused across the correction. Public pretrained backbones remain fixed references. TAPB workers use the dedicated TAPB environment; the additional native DrugBAN workers use their DGL-compatible environment. Qwen, proteomics and cell adapters use the LDA environment. They execute the same versioned optimization and aggregation source, not identical dependency binaries.

\paragraph{Complete common design library.}
Each row defines an executable candidate. D00 is the fixed starting recipe; D01--D11 are available without within-arm repeats. The six-slot budget does not exhaustively evaluate the library. FedProx is disabled in this version: for one-batch local clients its penalty has zero gradient at the sole step from the shared anchor, so a proximal-only change would be a no-op in the small tasks.

\paragraph{Generation and evidence.}
The local proposer uses bfloat16 inference, temperature 0.7, top-$p$ 0.9 and at most 512 new tokens. Slot $s$ uses seed $\text{training seed}+s$; a second bounded formatting attempt adds 10,000. The first direct proposal is copied to the paired loop arm rather than regenerated. Later direct proposals see configuration identities but no scores. Loop evidence contains the aggregate best development metric/loss, best round, first/last validation records, training losses and acceptance status. Raw worker tracebacks remain in local failure receipts; the proposer receives a numerical-failure diagnosis. No test score is passed to either proposer.

For PTPC, increasing weight decay to 0.01 preserves AP and wins only through the lower BCE tie-break (0.521161 versus 0.521177). VCC retains the original design after all six alternatives fail to improve selection. Norman's lower-learning-rate residual head improves development Top-1 from 0.400 to 0.519; the loop reaches it one proposal earlier, but both modes finish with the same winner. Within each example, direct and loop cover the same six designs, and the loop's fits reuse their paired completed computations. The final selected checkpoints are identical. These traces explain tied final outcomes without establishing an overall search-efficiency advantage.

The executable design identifier is authoritative; schema validity does not validate the natural-language rationale. In the retained PTPC/VCC/Norman examples, some Qwen hypotheses describe a residual adapter while selecting D04/D05/D10, which have no residual. We therefore do not treat these narratives as reliable mechanistic explanations. Single-client VCC seed 43 also has an invalid loop proposal that consumes a slot and leaves D05 untried; its different final design is not unambiguous evidence of better feedback reasoning. Exact proposal paths, configurations and checkpoint hashes are indexed in the repository's provenance record.

\paragraph{State-buffer regression.}
The paired five-round native DrugBAN regression fixes seed 42 and the D07 configuration. The old core produces negative BatchNorm running variances (including $-8.585$ and $-28.029$ in graph layers) and non-finite predictions. The corrected core keeps all recorded running variances nonnegative and completes scoring. Its five-round AUROC is not used as a scientific result, nor is this a hyperparameter-selection experiment. This check isolates the implementation failure that stopped the historical native queue.

\paragraph{Client and split integrity.}
DTI clients contain 4,932--5,058 training and 517--643 development pairs. Their compounds are disjoint across clients; within-client original train/development compound overlap is retained explicitly. PTPC compound groups do not cross clients or its newly reserved training/development boundary. Its development panel has 105 conditions, with small per-client positive counts. Cell clients have disjoint intervention identities and reuse the data construction in Appendix Q, but their new training uses batch 64, 100 rounds and MSE plus local contrastive loss rather than the old batch-two 20-round recipe. Common development reports are generated only after arm selection and are not loop feedback.

\paragraph{Current proteomic head and preprocessing.}
The current neural adapter preserves the original historical 491-condition source pool but partitions it into 386 training and 105 development conditions by compound group. For each observed 6h and 24h profile separately, it applies log1p, within-profile min--max normalization over finite observations, zero filling for missing entries and unit L2 normalization. These are per-observation operations, not pooled train-fitted statistics. The 2,048-bit Morgan vector is L2-normalized and multiplied by 0.5. The concatenated 12,390-dimensional vector (5,171 + 5,171 + 2,048) is multiplied by $3/\sqrt{1.25}$ and stored as float32. The adapter feeds these blocks to the ProteinTalks-derived efficacy head, with 464,193 parameters at the fixed design. All six arms use identical inputs, random initialization per seed, the common 100-round training budget and development-selected checkpoint rule. This differs from the median-imputed, 100-epoch historical source-editing protocol in Appendix C and from the separately retained linear control.

\paragraph{Completion and missing values.}
The scalar snapshot records which prespecified seeds have completed training and held-out scoring. The held-out exporter requires six selected arms, a checkpoint seal and independent verification; a completed development run alone cannot populate a test cell. Means include every available prespecified seed, with sample SD where defined. Missing models, seeds or evaluation roles remain N/A. An independent evaluator follows completed studies without sending test evidence to the research controller. Historical Appendix L/P/Q scores retain their original roles and protocols. Client rotations and same-data centralized comparisons remain separate requirements.

\paragraph{Additional DTI uncertainty analysis.}
Paired cluster-bootstrap intervals were added after training and held-out scoring, using retained predictions without model reselection. We use 1,000 resamples with a fixed analysis seed, grouping by the released drug identifier on random/unseen-drug endpoints and by protein sequence on unseen-protein. Each resample is shared by the compared methods. The full table includes AUROC and AP for all three endpoints, including zero and negative contrasts. These intervals condition on the fitted models and the chosen grouping; they do not capture between-training-seed variation or simultaneous drug/protein dependence. We report every seed rather than collapsing the sign-changing unseen-entity effects into the main random-split mean. A positive average should not be read as uniform transfer improvement.

\paragraph{Verification and publication.}
CPU unit/integration tests cover the common core, adapters, proposal generation, frozen evaluation and cell-mask correction. Publication tests check table values, missingness, ties, model identity and independent-verification bindings. The independent V3 audit validates all six selected arms, checkpoint existence, source hashes, training budgets, shared first proposals, evidence histories and common-panel metric aggregation. The paper snapshot includes receipt hashes and aggregate scalar metrics, not private biological arrays. A frozen export date prevents later-running experiments from silently changing the submitted PDF; refreshed exports must update the narrative and rerun compilation.

The corrected VCC seed-42 run has a pre-decoding core/checkpoint/data seal and an explicitly post-hoc supplemental wrapper-registration integrity receipt; it is not represented as a retroactive pre-registration. Later corrected-cell runs persist the wrapper registration before response decoding. The aggregate snapshot preserves this distinction. Environment and Qwen-asset hashes captured after some runs began are observational metadata, not advance registration.
