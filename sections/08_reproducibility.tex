\section*{Ethics statement}
This computational study uses previously released biological resources under their source access conditions and collects no new measurements or participant data. The experiments simulate separate laboratories on one host. Retaining raw data at each laboratory avoids direct transfer to the research model; parameter updates and aggregate diagnostics would still require an explicit privacy and security policy in a multi-institutional deployment. Predictions are evaluated as research outputs, not as validated biological or therapeutic interventions.
