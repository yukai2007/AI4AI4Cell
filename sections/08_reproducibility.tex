\section*{Reproducibility and responsible use}
The uniform implementation is versioned as \texttt{extensions/unified\_bio\_20260918} in the local research workspace. Definition files bind the common core, adapters, researcher, runner, data manifest, training seed and budget. Retained artifacts include every proposal and repair, accepted/rejected/failed history, training progress, selected checkpoints and post-selection development reports. The manuscript's generated scalar snapshot records completed studies and their source hashes; incomplete results stay N/A. Its generator is included under \texttt{tools/} and requires the separately held experimental result archive, not raw biological responses, to refresh the tables.

The writing repository is not a standalone benchmark release: it does not contain full datasets, private response shards, model checkpoints or dependency-complete runtime environments. Offline packaging occurs on one host, and neither secure aggregation nor differential privacy is implemented. Existing public test roles were previously examined in historical studies; newly completed runs must distinguish retrospective benchmark scoring from independent blind validation. These experiments are not prospective wet-lab or clinical studies, and predictions are not validated therapeutic advice.

\section*{Ethics statement}
This retrospective computational study collects no new biological measurements and enrolls no human participants. We use released benchmark data under their source access conditions and do not redistribute restricted measurements in the manuscript repository. The ten-client partitions simulate institutional data boundaries; they do not establish a formal privacy guarantee. In particular, the current implementation provides neither secure aggregation nor differential privacy, and its predictions must not be used for clinical or therapeutic decisions without independent validation.
