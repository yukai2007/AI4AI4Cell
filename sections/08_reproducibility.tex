\section*{Reproducibility and responsible use}
The uniform implementation is versioned as \texttt{extensions/unified\_bio\_20260918}. Definition files bind the common core, task adapters, proposer, runner, data manifest, training seed and budget. For every proposal, the artifact records the structured request, executable configuration, accepted/rejected/failed outcome, training progress, selected checkpoint and aggregate development report. The racing study additionally records the complete short-run slate, promotion decision, full-validation set, equal-compute assertion and independently rescored held-out predictions. The manuscript snapshot records source hashes and the executed scalar results used to generate its tables.

The three task adapters use released biological resources under their source access conditions. Raw response arrays and checkpoints remain in the experimental workspace; the writing repository contains aggregate results, provenance records and table-generation code. The ten workers simulate laboratory data holders on one host and communicate through the same update/evidence interfaces that can be deployed across institutions. The present implementation uses standard sample-weighted aggregation; secure aggregation and differential privacy can be attached to this interface in deployments requiring formal protection.

\section*{Ethics statement}
This retrospective computational study collects no new biological measurements and enrolls no human participants. The ten-client partitions simulate institutional data boundaries. Predictions are research outputs for benchmark evaluation and require independent experimental validation before biological or therapeutic use.
