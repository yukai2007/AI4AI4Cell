\section*{Reproducibility statement}
Section 3 specifies the research and training interfaces; Section 4 defines task inputs, data partitions, reference models and metrics. Appendix B specifies the laboratory-count, source-transfer and proposer-budget studies. The accompanying Statistical Supplement gives complete per-seed contrasts and confidence intervals for every reported contrast, including non-positive differences. An anonymized release accompanies the submission and contains the implementation, task adapters, experiment configurations, the full proposal log and the prediction-level evaluation records, bound to code and data hashes, random seeds, development trajectories, selection decisions and saved predictions. The proposal log records all 504 attempted slots ($20\times6$ for the six-slot protocol plus $16\times24$ for the extended protocol) with their configuration changes; the appendix presents representative decisions and complete performance summaries. Aggregate tables are generated from these completed records, with independent score recomputation for the main comparison and completed follow-up evaluations. Two limits are stated explicitly: the local Qwen block is seed-reproducible, while the Luna service exposes no generation seed and is not bit-reproducible, and the public self-improving agents are task-adapted re-implementations whose disabled native components are listed in Appendix~C.

\section*{Ethics statement}
This computational study uses previously released biological resources under their source access conditions and collects no new measurements or participant data. The experiments simulate separate laboratories on one host. Retaining raw data at each laboratory avoids direct transfer to the self-improving model; parameter updates and aggregate diagnostics would still require an explicit privacy and security policy in a multi-institutional deployment. Predictions are evaluated as research outputs, not as validated biological or therapeutic interventions.
