\section*{6. Discussion and limitations}
The empirical distinction is between three properties: an artifact can be auditable, a new information source can be useful, and a controller can improve held-out utility. None implies the others. The cell results show a small full-system improvement with important confounds; the fixed-policy replay shows the value of more calibration information without a distributed numerical penalty; the DTI comparison tests native controller choices with a common agenda and genuinely withheld final scores. Neither primary comparison meets the predeclared superiority criterion. The positive means therefore describe a small, seed-dependent gain under this budget.

Our DTI budget is intentionally limited to one task family, five campaigns per controller, and short search fits followed by a fixed larger refit. It cannot establish superiority over DrugEvolve's full publication, broad task coverage, or current DTI state of the art. We did not tune the original DrugBAN domain-adaptation protocol or reconstruct undisclosed paper-specific evolved kwargs. Cold tests use exact identities, not scaffold/protein-family exclusion. The study lacks an independent laboratory-level test, a second new biological endpoint, and causal memory/hypothesis ablations. These absences limit the claims; they are not filled by counting more test splits as more tasks.

The federation provides a concrete data-flow and evidence interface, but the present biological clients are simulated shards. Matched-interface search comparisons, realistic heterogeneity, source-level participation, privacy accounting, and malicious-client behavior require separate experiments. Harness-free and DrugEvolve can also be connected to local-data interfaces. The defensible hypothesis is that explicit artifact contracts and evidence lineage improve coordination, not that alternative controllers are fundamentally unable to preserve data locality.

A perturbation-proteomics extension based on ProteinTalks \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks} is being prepared. Public supplementary datasets have been obtained, but the task adapter and prospective evaluation are not yet complete. No proteomics result is included here. A prospective matched-interface study remains necessary to separate the research-loop effect from the granularity of client evidence.
