\section*{6. Discussion: what improves the biological predictor?}
The experiments separate predictive design, training-data access and research organization. Compact prediction programs improve cold-test ranking when transferred from DrugBAN to TAPB without another fit or search. Most of the gain comes from a shared mixture, but its portability remains useful: the deployable result is executable code. Native trajectories explain how failures influence proposals; matched comparisons test whether that process improves prediction beyond direct generation.

Federation expands the measurements available to fit a shared design. With fixed features, ten-client proteomics AP and AUROC improve over one-client means while matching the centralized objective numerically. Direct generation can use the same service: comparing ten-client loop research only with one-client direct would confound organization with data access. Our factorial study separates these effects. The deterministic same-host shards test computation and data availability, not the willingness of ten institutions to share measurements.

Evaluation quality is a research bottleneck. The initial development/gate roles contain eight and nine positives; training-only CV covers 86 positives with 26--31 per fold. The follow-up improves loop AP descriptively, but direct still has the highest AP and controller contrasts remain inconclusive. Visible-score improvement does not guarantee final-role generalization. The loop and direct share the numerical selector, so their contrast tests research organization, not gate-on versus gate-off. Independent-study or held-out-client validation, including worst-client performance, and a matched gate ablation would distinguish generalization from selection effects.

The scope remains task-specific: BAN-module search and pretrained TAPB have different representation budgets; exact-identity coldness does not imply scaffold or protein-family separation. PTPC predicts efficacy from observed responses, not untreated-cell outcomes or the complete ProteinTalks neural-ODE model \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}. Local execution supports data-local collaboration; formal privacy, malicious-client robustness and independent institutional deployment require additional mechanisms and validation.
