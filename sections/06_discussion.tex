\section*{6. Discussion}
The central contribution is a common route for biological laboratories to contribute to model development at two levels: fitted parameters and evidence for revising computational designs. The framework can operate when measurements remain with their data-owning workers. Reusing one training and research implementation across DTI, proteomics and cell perturbations holds the orchestration and feedback intervention constant while exposing task-dependent outcomes. It does not make the datasets, model capacities or difficulty interchangeable.

The completed held-out comparisons show that this interface is applicable, but they also make the bridging difficulty concrete. Additional laboratory participation improves the loop comparison on all five reported endpoints, while expanding both training and selection data. At matched access, the six-proposal endpoint ties because direct and iterative search often try the same candidates and select the same checkpoint. The complete prefix analysis nevertheless shows an early two-proposal advantage in two of fifteen trajectories and no disadvantage at that budget; the open-loop controller catches up as the small twelve-design library saturates. The next decisive test is therefore a prespecified, non-saturating design space with anytime regret and frozen held-out performance, rather than a larger language model alone. One-laboratory design search can also select revisions that worsen held-out scores relative to a fixed recipe, so cross-laboratory evidence remains central to proposal evaluation.

Several limits remain specific and actionable. Proteomics and cell development panels contain few positive compounds or interventions per client, which makes local proposal evaluation fragile. The cell backbone is frozen, Tahoe uses a custom chemical adapter, and perturbation identification is not equivalent to forward-expression prediction. Laboratory partitions are simulated rather than independently collected cohorts, and the current one-laboratory reference is client 0 rather than a rotation over laboratories. Final benchmark roles used historically are not newly blinded. Strong same-data centralized references, independent-study evaluation and formal privacy mechanisms are additional validation requirements, not supplied by a positive participation contrast.
