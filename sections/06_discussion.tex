\section*{6. Discussion: what improves the biological predictor?}
The experiments separate predictive design, access to training measurements and research organization. A compact prediction program transfers from DrugBAN to a larger pretrained TAPB backbone, improving cold-test ranking without another fit or search. This portability is useful even when a simple shared mixture supplies most of the gain: the deployable result is executable code, not a model-specific narrative. Direct generation is a strong comparator under the same source and evidence contract. Native trajectories show how failures influence later proposals; full held-out comparisons measure whether that additional process improves prediction.

Federation changes which locally held measurements can contribute to fitting a shared design. In the fixed-feature proteomics study, the ten-client model improves final AP and AUROC over the mean one-client model while matching the centralized objective numerically. Direct generation can use the same local-training service. Thus a comparison of ten-client loop research with one-client direct generation alone would confound research organization with data availability. The factorial study separates them: additional access improves several prediction metrics, but the development-gated loop does not outperform direct at equal access. The participants are deterministic same-host shards, not ten recruited institutions; their organization tests computation and data access, not an institutional willingness-to-share model.

Evaluation quality is itself a research bottleneck. The original proteomics development/gate roles contain eight and nine positives, and all final logistic predictions remain negative at the fixed threshold despite improved ranking. Training-only CV covers all 86 training positives through three compound-disjoint folds, with 26--31 positives per fold. The completed follow-up improves the loop's mean final AP descriptively, but direct generation still has the highest AP and paired controller contrasts remain inconclusive. Representation research is feasible with broader training-only evidence, but this comparison does not isolate the effect of CV selection. Independent-source validation would provide stronger generalization evidence than further development on these previously exposed public final roles.

The biological and computational scope is explicit. DTI architecture search changes only the BAN module; the full-training follow-up measures transfer of those selected architectures, while the larger pretrained TAPB model tests predictive strength under a different representation budget. Exact-identity coldness is not scaffold or protein-family separation. PTPC predicts efficacy from already observed protein responses, not an untreated-cell forecast or a reproduction of the complete ProteinTalks neural-ODE model \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}. Local execution and bounded evidence support review and data-local collaboration, but formal privacy, malicious-client robustness and independently administered deployment require additional mechanisms and validation.
