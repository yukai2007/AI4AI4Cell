\section*{6. Discussion: what improves the biological predictor?}
The experiments separate predictive design, training-data access and research organization. Stronger retrieval and proteomics feature maps raise the achievable scores; sharing these starts with both controllers makes their contributions identifiable. Compact prediction programs also improve cold-test ranking when transferred from DrugBAN to TAPB without another fit or search. The deployable result is reusable code. Native trajectories explain how evidence influences proposals; matched comparisons test whether that process improves prediction beyond direct generation.

Federation expands the measurements available to fit a shared design. With fixed features, ten-client proteomics AP and AUROC improve over one-client means while matching the centralized objective numerically. Direct generation can use the same service: comparing ten-client loop research only with one-client direct would confound organization with data access. Our factorial study separates these effects. The deterministic same-host shards test computation and data availability, not the willingness of ten institutions to share measurements.

Evaluation quality is a research bottleneck. The initial development/gate roles contain eight and nine positives; training-only CV covers 86 positives with 26--31 per fold. The new strong-start loop has slightly higher primary AP than direct, whereas the earlier CV comparison favored direct; neither establishes a controller ranking. The fixed strong start also retains better PTPC AUROC and BCE than subsequent research. Visible-score improvement need not generalize across metrics or final examples. Because both controllers share the numerical selector, their contrast tests research organization, not gate-on versus gate-off. Independent-study or held-out-client validation, including worst-client performance, and a matched gate ablation would strengthen these conclusions.

The scope remains task-specific: BAN-module search and pretrained TAPB have different representation budgets; exact-identity coldness does not imply scaffold or protein-family separation. PTPC predicts efficacy from observed responses, not untreated-cell outcomes or the complete ProteinTalks neural-ODE model \citep{Sun2026ProteinTalks}. Local execution supports data-local collaboration; formal privacy, malicious-client robustness and independent institutional deployment require additional mechanisms and validation.
