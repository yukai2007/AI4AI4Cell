\subsection*{5.2 Separating laboratory participation from research feedback}
With the loop held fixed, expanding participation from one to ten laboratories improves mean-client proteomic AP from 54.97 to 67.15, a 12.18-point development gain. The analogous cell gains are 11.80 points for VCC, 29.30 for Norman doubles and 13.33 for Tahoe drugs (Table~\ref{tab:unified-effects}). These comparisons demonstrate improved use of additional locally held measurements in this first seed; they do not identify an intrinsic federated-optimizer advantage at matched data volume.

Comparing ten-laboratory loop with ten-laboratory fixed training, VCC improves from 13.30\% to 30.08\% macro Top-1 and Tahoe from 28.33\% to 31.67\%. Proteomics and Norman tie with their fixed references on the primary endpoint. However, matched-budget direct search attains the same final primary scores as the loop in all four completed branches. Thus design search improves two branches, while an additional benefit specifically from feedback-guided iteration is not yet established. The expanded six-slot direct search also reaches the Tahoe design that exceeded the earlier three-slot direct control; the earlier 3.33-point feedback contrast is not substituted for the current zero contrast.

Proteomics shows a secondary difference despite tied AP: mean-client BCE is 0.4949 for loop versus 0.5020 for direct. AUROC and accuracy remain tied. Appendix R reports all secondary metrics, including this predictive-loss improvement, without replacing the prespecified AP endpoint.

Single-laboratory candidate selection uses only client 0, while the common report includes all ten development clients. Consequently selection can improve client-0 loss yet reduce the broader diagnostic score, as in proteomics and Tahoe. This is evidence for limited single-laboratory transfer, not a reason to reselect candidates on the common panel. Multiple training seeds and client rotations are needed to assess its stability.

\subsection*{5.3 Mechanism and execution evidence}
All completed branches share the same proposer, configuration library, local optimizer, aggregation rule and retention code. Stored proposal inputs verify that direct search receives no evaluation history; the paired first proposal is identical. Completed studies pass checks of six-arm completeness, 100-round fitting, checkpoint lineage, development aggregation and source consistency. Numerical failures consume slots and retain the incumbent.

A paired regression identifies why the earlier native DTI queue stopped: momentum was incorrectly applied to BatchNorm buffers, producing negative running variances and non-finite predictions. The common revised trainer applies momentum only to parameters. With the same seed and candidate, the five-round regression completes with nonnegative running variances. This is an implementation correction, not a predictive-performance gain; all new-protocol scientific fits restart independently rather than mixing the old and corrected checkpoints.

\subsection*{5.4 Supporting results from earlier protocols}
Earlier completed studies remain useful but answer different questions. Full-training architecture transfer yielded unseen-drug AUROC 0.9330 for loop-selected DrugBAN designs versus 0.9239 for native DrugEvolve and 0.9280 for direct generation, across five paired seeds (Appendix L). The stronger shared TAPB--retrieval design reached random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein AUROC 0.9710/0.9588/0.9317, with direct and loop tying from that same start (Appendix P).

The earlier local-gradient proteomics study increased fixed-design final AP from 0.3088 with one client to 0.3699 with ten; loop/direct AP was 0.3727/0.3716 from a shared strong start. Those results used a different fitter, partition and source-editing controller. Appendix P retains the complete paired comparisons and uncertainty; Appendix Q retains the prior 20-round cell pilots. They support predictor improvement and local-data access, but are not evidence that the present uniform framework has completed final testing or achieved a general loop advantage.
