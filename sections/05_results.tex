Additional laboratory participation is the most consistent final-budget effect. Holding the research loop fixed, ten-laboratory participation adds \StrongDTIFederationEffect{} AUROC points for DTI, \StrongPTPCFederationEffect{} AP points for proteomics, and \StrongVCCFederationEffect{}, \StrongNormanFederationEffect{} and \StrongTahoeFederationEffect{} Top-1 points for VCC, Norman and Tahoe. At ten-laboratory access, the complete loop also improves over the fixed recipe by \StrongDTISearchEffect{} points on DTI and \StrongNormanSearchEffect{} points on Norman, ties on VCC, and selects closely matched solutions on proteomics and Tahoe.

The proposal trajectory reveals where persistent evidence helps. After only two proposals, the loop is better than direct search in two of the 15 ten-laboratory task--seed trajectories, tied in thirteen and worse in none, yielding a mean development-primary advantage of 0.78 points. Norman seed 42 illustrates the mechanism: the evidence-guided loop reaches design D08 in slot 2, while direct search reaches the same design in slot 3. With six proposals, both modes frequently cover the same high-value configurations and converge to identical selected checkpoints. The present loop therefore improves early search efficiency in a subset of trajectories, while the compact twelve-design library makes the final budget easy for direct search to saturate.

The one-laboratory arms further show why proposal evaluation benefits from multiple sources of evidence: revisions selected from client 0 alone transfer less reliably on proteomics and the three cell strata. Appendix A reports the complete proposal prefixes, all seeds, DTI unseen-drug and unseen-protein endpoints, and paired bootstrap intervals.

\subsection*{5.3 Execution and research-history evidence}
All branches share the proposer, design library, fitting schedule, aggregator and finalizer. Their paired first proposal is identical; subsequent loop proposals additionally receive the accumulated evidence cards. Checkpoint seals, saved predictions and independent score recomputation connect every reported value to an executed run. The histories retain both successful and unsuccessful directions, so a selected design can be traced back through its hypothesis, configuration, development trajectory and retention decision.

Implementation checks exercise the same interfaces used by the scientific runs. The common trainer applies server momentum to parameters while aggregating model buffers by type. Five response-free tests verify the cell attention masks, including causal direction, padding exclusion, unchanged pretrained weights and BF16 execution, after which every cell arm is evaluated on the corrected features.
