\subsection*{4.4 Measured trajectories improve training allocation}
We apply the policy in Section 3.4 to the same ten designs and ten laboratories under both schedules. Each spends 800 aggregation rounds and 160 aggregate development evaluations: uniform allocation uses $10\times80$ rounds, while screening and promotion use $10\times20+6\times100$.

Trajectory-guided allocation moves Norman from 11.48\% to 22.32\% Top-1: a +10.84-point mean effect, positive in every seed, with hierarchical paired-bootstrap 95\% CI [4.54, 17.73]. Chance is 20\% on this five-option task, so the uniform schedule sits below chance and the evidence-guided schedule recovers above it, restoring usable signal rather than adding a large margin. Across all twelve task--seed comparisons (seeds 53--55) the policy gives four wins and eight ties; Tahoe gains 1.99 points on average, while VCC and proteomics tie. The VCC level here is not comparable with Table~\ref{tab:public-harness-comparison-three-seed} (31.54): this study uses its own menu and seeds 53--55, and VCC is where development selection transfers least well, its held-out Top-1 falling from 31.50 to 19.61 as the proposal budget grows from 6 to 24 slots (Appendix~B). The feedback channel's measurable held-out effect is therefore confined to allocation; under a fixed proposal budget the two search modes retain the same checkpoint.

\subsection*{4.5 Learning from different laboratory scenarios}
Source-as-laboratory experiments replace random shards with distinct biological collections: BindingDB, BioSNAP, Davis and Human for DTI, three external single-cell studies for VCC, and the target PTPC collection plus Lin, Ruprecht and decryptE for proteomics \citep{Lin2026HDAC,Ruprecht2020Proteome,Eckert2024DecryptE}. The target's own development panels select checkpoints and a common target test measures transfer; Appendix~B gives identities, sizes, exclusions and the full source-composition comparison. External measurements can improve target prediction: adding all three proteomic studies raises AP from 34.41 to 37.53, and Davis and Human raise DTI AUROC from 85.47 to 87.86 and 87.95. Source compatibility nevertheless matters alongside data volume, and at matched participation and condition count heterogeneous updates do not improve VCC identification (28.82\% versus 30.70\%).
