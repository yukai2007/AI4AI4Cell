\subsection*{5.2 Separating participation, search and feedback}
Table~\ref{tab:strong-effects} isolates the value of iterative evidence under matched data, candidate and compute budgets. It compares uniform feedback-free allocation with the screening--promotion--validation loop; both arms use ten laboratories and the same candidate slate.

\input{tables/strong_v3/effects}

The compute-matched loop is never worse on the 12 newly executed held-out task--seed evaluations: it wins four and ties eight. The largest effect is on Norman double-gene perturbations, where the held-out macro Top-1 mean increases from 11.48\% to 22.32\%, a +10.84-point effect with hierarchical paired-bootstrap 95\% CI [4.54, 17.73]. Tahoe improves by +1.99 points on average, with one win and two ties; VCC and proteomics tie exactly. On DTI, a conservative replay of previously completed development trajectories gives the feedback-free arm slightly more candidate-rounds (525 versus 520), yet the loop wins two seeds, ties one and adds 0.39 AUROC points on average. This replay is reported as development evidence rather than a new held-out result.

The gain comes from allocation rather than additional designs or measurements. Uniform search spends the same 80-round budget on every candidate. The loop uses short trajectories to retain both strong current candidates and candidates that are still improving, then concentrates full validation on that promoted set. On Norman, the promoted low-learning-rate residual design continues improving after the uniform arm stops; the resulting checkpoint improves held-out performance in all three seeds. The exact ties on VCC and proteomics show that racing preserves the strongest uniform-search solution when additional validation is unnecessary.

The original factorial experiment remains useful for separating participation and design search. Holding the research loop fixed, ten-laboratory participation adds \StrongDTIFederationEffect{} AUROC points for DTI, \StrongPTPCFederationEffect{} AP points for proteomics, and \StrongVCCFederationEffect{}, \StrongNormanFederationEffect{} and \StrongTahoeFederationEffect{} Top-1 points for VCC, Norman and Tahoe. Appendix A reports the complete six-arm results, proposal prefixes, secondary metrics, DTI unseen-entity endpoints and paired bootstrap intervals.

\subsection*{5.3 Execution and research-history evidence}
All branches share the proposer, design library, fitting schedule, aggregator and finalizer. Their paired first proposal is identical; subsequent loop proposals additionally receive the accumulated evidence cards. Checkpoint seals, saved predictions and independent score recomputation connect every reported value to an executed run. The histories retain both successful and unsuccessful directions, so a selected design can be traced back through its hypothesis, configuration, development trajectory and retention decision.

Implementation checks exercise the same interfaces used by the scientific runs. The common trainer applies server momentum to parameters while aggregating model buffers by type. Five response-free tests verify the cell attention masks, including causal direction, padding exclusion, unchanged pretrained weights and BF16 execution, after which every cell arm is evaluated on the corrected features.
