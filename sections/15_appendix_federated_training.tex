\section*{Appendix K. Local training and data availability}
\label{app:local-training}
The federated-training extension adds an inner task-weight optimization to the outer artifact-research loop. This is distinct from the earlier PTPC partner study, whose clients only evaluated centrally fitted candidates. A candidate specifies a row-wise, parameter-free feature map $\phi_a$; every participating training client runs the same source on its own measurements. The research model receives source and aggregate evaluation diagnostics, not the client arrays or optimizer gradients.

For participating clients $\mathcal K$, let $z_{ij}=w^\top\phi_a(x_{ij})+b$ and $N=\sum_{i\in\mathcal K}n_i$. The fixed logistic fitter minimizes
\[
 F_a(w,b)=\frac{1}{N}\left[\sum_{i\in\mathcal K}\sum_{j=1}^{n_i}
 \left\{\log(1+e^{z_{ij}})-y_{ij}z_{ij}\right\}
 +\frac{\|w\|_2^2}{2C}\right],\qquad C=1.
\]
Each client returns its summed logistic loss, gradient and row count. A trusted coordinator sums these terms, adds the coefficient penalty once, and evaluates the fixed L-BFGS-B update. The intercept is unpenalized. Initialization is zero; the iteration ceiling is 1,000, with gradient tolerance $10^{-9}$, function tolerance $10^{-15}$, maximum line-search steps 50 and history size 20. Clients are separate same-host processes, not independently administered laboratories. The optimizer coordinator does not load their raw arrays; the outer trusted dispatch service nevertheless reads shard bytes to verify their hashes. Aggregate-only optimization is therefore not a claim that the entire orchestrator is denied filesystem access to the data. Gradient exchange is not differentially private or securely aggregated.

\paragraph{Equivalence check.}
For a fixed feature map and identical participating rows, the summed local objective and gradient equal their centralized counterparts in exact arithmetic. This follows by additivity of the per-example loss and its derivative; it is an implementation property, not a new federated optimizer. It isolates data availability from a change in the fitting objective. Across all 15 client-pool/partition combinations, centralized and distributed fits converge, with maximum coefficient difference $3.162\times10^{-9}$ and maximum development-probability difference $4.422\times10^{-10}$.

\paragraph{Fixed-feature development study.}
Five label-independent compound partitions (seeds 442--446) divide the original 491 training conditions into ten clients. One-client and three-client conditions use the first one or three clients; the ten-client condition uses all 491 rows. Each configuration is fitted both centrally and through local objective workers, giving 30 fits. No gate, partner or final arrays are opened in this study. The fixed preprocessing applies observed-value log1p and within-profile min--max scaling, then zero-fills missing values. The feature map L2-normalizes the flattened two-time protein block and drug block separately, concatenates them, and L2-normalizes the result. It has 12,390 features and no pooled preprocessing statistics.

\subsection*{K.1 Matched research and data-access study}
The separate feature-search study crosses direct generation and the actual production loop with one or ten training clients, using the same five compound partitions 442--446. It declares 20 campaigns before execution. Both controllers start from the fixed feature map above and use the identical local-gradient fitter. The 1-client training pools contain 59, 58, 48, 44 and 45 conditions; every 10-client pool contains all 491 conditions. Partitioning changes the one-client training population, while 10-client trajectories differ in research generation and numerical summation order, not available examples.

Only a stateless NumPy function of preprocessed protein and compound inputs can change. Its output has at most 25,000 features; source is limited to 120 KB. Row outputs must be invariant to order, batch size, added rows and singleton execution, with no input mutation or label/file/network access. The numerical allowlist includes array arithmetic, reductions and norms but excludes random state, Fourier-transform namespaces and fitting utilities. These are implementation limits, not biological constraints. In particular, fixed block scaling changes the effective regularization at fixed $C=1$; an improved feature weighting alone is not evidence of a biological mechanism.

Every campaign has three candidate opportunities, four fit attempts including baseline and a 24-call research ceiling. Direct emits three independent source proposals from the common baseline and baseline evidence. The loop retains native proposal, implementation, repair, measurement, retention and RunNode-history execution through the task adapter. Both use strict simultaneous development and visible-gate AP improvement above $10^{-4}$; subjective native assessments are advisory. Failed calls and invalid candidates remain charged. A legal retained incumbent is required for every campaign; failure cannot remove a seed from the table.

The development and visible-gate roles contain 96/90 conditions and 8/9 positives. The 154-condition partner role is unused here. All 20 source/parameter selections and budgets must close before the final 148-condition, 22-positive role is opened. Deployment reuses the selected local-training parameters without another fit. The final roster also includes each direct campaign's already-fitted unmodified baseline, giving six arms and 30 method/partition records with no extra model selection.

Final AP is primary. The two frozen contrasts are loop-10 minus direct-10 and loop-10 minus loop-1. Two-sided exact sign-flip tests enumerate all 32 assignments and use Holm correction across these two contrasts. Descriptive paired bootstrap intervals use 10,000 draws with seed 20260913. They condition on the one historical compound split and the five declared partitions, not a population of laboratories. These public final labels were evaluated in the earlier neural-head study; the new feature study keeps them outside its search service but does not claim a freshly blind benchmark.

\subsection*{K.2 Completed final comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:federated-research} reports the complete six-arm study and the two additional matched cosine references.

All 20 campaigns complete and all 30 declared final records are retained, with 24 unique prediction identities. The fixed ten-client model reaches AP 0.2783 and AUROC 0.7273, compared with one-client means of 0.2431 and 0.6574. These are final-set data-access gains of 0.0352 AP and 0.0699 AUROC for the unchanged feature map, smaller than its development AP gain. Both controllers can use the additional local training data: direct ten-client AP is 0.2974 and loop ten-client AP is 0.2696.

The primary loop-10 minus direct-10 AP difference is negative in all five partitions, with mean $-0.0278$, descriptive 95\% bootstrap interval $[-0.0545,-0.0083]$, exact $p=0.0625$ and Holm-adjusted $p=0.125$. Loop-10 minus loop-1 is $+0.0112$, with interval $[-0.0358,+0.0554]$ and exact/Holm $p=0.6875$. The first result does not support the intended loop advantage; the second is an uncertain primary-AP data-access effect within the loop. Secondary mean AUROC rises from 0.6556 to 0.7219 and BCE falls from 0.4229 to 0.3914 within the loop. All 30 logistic predictions classify every final condition as negative at 0.5; their accuracy 0.85135 is therefore the majority-class reference, not demonstrated positive-case detection.

Every saved final vector and all 150 successful development/gate vectors reproduce the original scorer exactly. Actual costs are 75 successful and one failed fit, 166 LLM calls, 19 retained changes, 36 rejections and five failed candidates. Four failures occur before fitting under the numerical API allowlist; one fitting failure attempts to modify a read-only input. Native repair uses 17 calls, all charged, and all 30 native round decisions persist. Direct uses 15 calls per client-count arm; loop uses 63 at one client and 73 at ten clients. Training exchanges 12,569 RPC replies, with 1,542,861,751 request and 1,543,653,598 reply bytes of serialized application payloads. These are measured protocol payloads, not network-traffic measurements or communication savings.

\paragraph{Same-pool cosine reference.}
The additional deterministic references use these exact one-client and ten-client training pools, the same fixed feature map, cosine five-neighbor averaging and condition-key tie breaking. There is no tuning of features, neighbor count or threshold on the final set. They are centrally computed fixed references added after the research study, not a claim of private nearest-neighbor inference. All ten saved vectors reproduce the original scorer, and all five full-pool prediction vectors are identical. Full-pool cosine AP/AUROC/BCE are 0.1996/0.6153/1.3152. An administrative recovery allowed the ten declared prediction-output paths through an input-only file guard; the numerical runner and frozen recipe were unchanged.

\subsection*{K.3 A complete hypothesis--measurement--revision trajectory}
The first declared loop campaign, partition 442 with one training client, provides an outcome-independent case. Its starting feature map flattens the two observed timepoints before normalization. The first hypothesis asks whether separately normalized temporal mean and 24h-minus-6h difference blocks better represent response level and change under the fixed regularized fitter. The implemented change preserves the protein feature dimension while altering its geometry. Both visible AP values improve, so the source is retained.
