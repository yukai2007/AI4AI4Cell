\paragraph{Collaborative scientific agents and automated federated design.}
The Virtual Biotech coordinates specialist agents to integrate biomedical evidence for therapeutic decisions \citep{Zhang2026VirtualBiotech}. AutoScientists organizes research teams around shared experimental state, including successful and failed trials \citep{Gao2026AutoScientists}. Helmsman is more directly related: it generates federated-learning systems through planning, modular coding and simulation-based refinement \citep{Li2025Helmsman}. BioCoLoop focuses on an operational interface through which laboratories contribute local parameter updates and development measurements to biological predictor development. Its controlled design space makes it possible to assess laboratory participation, proposal-history feedback and training allocation separately. This distinction concerns the evaluated research interface and its biological evidence, rather than the invention of multi-agent coordination or automated federated design.
