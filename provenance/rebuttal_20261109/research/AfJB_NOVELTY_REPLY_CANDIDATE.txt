Thank you for identifying the relevant connections. We have expanded the related-work discussion
to distinguish collaboration among research agents from collaboration among data holders, while
recognizing that Helmsman already addresses automated federated-system design. The Virtual
Biotech coordinates specialist biomedical analyses; AutoScientists studies self-organizing
research teams with shared experimental histories; and Helmsman generates and refines federated
learning code. BioCoLoop's focus is a common experimental interface for biological predictors:
local parameter fitting and local development evaluation supply comparable evidence for choosing
configurations and allocating training. Our experiments deliberately use a bounded configuration
library so that these decisions can be evaluated under controlled access and budgets. We have
made this scope explicit and separated the combined-system comparison from the evidence for
each component. We agree that the ten-versus-one-laboratory main table alone cannot establish
an advantage of proposal history; the new same-access comparison addresses that question directly.
