# Citation and submission-requirement audit

- Review question: Do all actually cited references have correct identities and support their adjacent claims, and what ICLR requirements are verifiable from official guidance?
- Intended reader: manuscript authors addressing coauthor annotations.
- Retrieval cutoff: 2026-09-22 (session date).
- Unit of analysis: individual bibliography item, adjacent manuscript claim, official conference requirement.
- Meaning of all: every bibliography entry cited by the current manuscript; uncited entries are marked separately.
- Include: versioned papers, publisher metadata and full text, official repositories, official ICLR author guidance.
- Exclude: secondary summaries as final evidence; unsupported interpretation of current conference rules from an earlier edition.
- Comparison axes: title/authors/year/identifier/version; claim support; primary-source accessibility; required versus recommended statements and formatting.
- Known access limits: live publisher access may fail; unavailable full text is recorded as pending rather than represented as verified. The literature-review skill's sibling paper-writing and general-writing packages are unavailable locally.
- Deliverables: independent citation audit report and evidence rows in this directory; no changes to shared manuscript or bibliography by the auditor.
