\title{AI4AI4Cell: An Auditable Harness for Evidence-Gated Agentic Research in Cell Biology}
\author{Anonymous}
\date{}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We study AI4AI4Cell, an executable research interface for biological algorithm search: hypotheses produce versioned code, fixed local evaluators guide retention, and subsequent proposals consume the resulting evidence. Its federated-evidence extension contributes local diagnostics without changing language-model weights or placing raw measurement matrices in the model prompt. In exploratory cell-policy comparisons, the production harness attains 41/51 reverse-identification hits versus 39/51 for direct generation, a small sequential-score increase, and a tie on a third task; the same-data federated variant does not improve the non-federated result. A fixed-policy replay isolates calibration-information access: 30 rather than 3 conditions improves visible-gate accuracy by 4.31 percentage points over the mean single-client result, with numerical agreement between aggregated and centralized sufficient statistics. We then compare harness-free generation, native DrugEvolve, and our native harness on drug-target interaction prediction using five campaigns per method, a common research agenda and resource ceilings, and three held-out evaluation regimes. On the predeclared unseen-drug endpoint, our harness reaches AUROC 0.8922; its paired mean differences are +0.0057 versus native DrugEvolve; +0.0069 versus harness-free generation. Both paired confidence intervals include zero. These experiments separate measured utility, information access, and traceability; the local-data interface does not itself provide a formal privacy guarantee.

\end{abstract}
