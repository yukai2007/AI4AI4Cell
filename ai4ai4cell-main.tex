\title{AI4AI4Cell: Federated Evidence-Guided Research for Biological Model Improvement}
\author{Anonymous}
\date{}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Biological laboratories hold complementary measurements that could improve shared models without centralizing the underlying data. We present AI4AI4Cell, a federated research framework coupling executable-design revision with client-local parameter fitting. A fixed language model proposes and implements targeted changes; local trainers fit task weights, and aggregate evaluation evidence guides subsequent revisions. On the original DrugEvolve DTI splits, 100-epoch refits of loop-selected architectures improve mean unseen-drug AUROC by 0.0091 over native DrugEvolve. Combining a stronger backbone with development-selected retrieval raises random/unseen-drug/unseen-protein AUROC to 0.9710/0.9588/0.9317. In observed-response proteomics, expanding a fixed design from one to ten simulated clients raises mean final average precision from 0.3088 to 0.3699; native loop research reaches 0.3727. Twenty matched strong-start campaigns give both controllers identical improved designs: DTI ties direct generation, while proteomics exceeds its 0.3716 mean by 0.0011. These controls attribute the substantial gains to predictive design and local-data access, with a smaller, variable contribution from research organization. Across these tasks and exploratory cell policies, AI4AI4Cell lets locally held biological evidence improve both a shared executable design and its fitted parameters.
\end{abstract}
