\title{AI4AI4Cell: Federated Evidence-Guided Research for Biological Model Improvement}
\author{Anonymous}
\date{}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
Biological model improvement requires learning from fragmented experimental data and translating research ideas into reusable computational designs. We present AI4AI4Cell, a federated research framework coupling two levels of improvement: executable-design revision and client-local parameter fitting. A fixed language model proposes and implements targeted changes; measurements from accepted and rejected candidates inform subsequent revisions. Local trainers fit task weights through objective and gradient aggregation, while local evaluators return summaries to the research loop. On the original DrugEvolve DTI splits, 100-epoch refits of our selected architectures improve mean unseen-drug AUROC by 0.0091 over native DrugEvolve. Frozen prediction programs selected with DrugBAN further transfer to TAPB without additional training or search, raising unseen-drug/unseen-protein AUROC from 0.9328/0.8868 to 0.9512/0.9149. In observed-response proteomics, expanding unchanged-model access from one to ten simulated clients raises mean final AP from 0.2431 to 0.2783; CV-guided loop research reaches 0.3111. Matched controls attribute most program-transfer gains to predictor combination and show that loop organization does not consistently outperform direct generation. Across these tasks and exploratory cell policies, AI4AI4Cell makes model-design changes transferable and enables locally held measurements to contribute to their training and evaluation.
\end{abstract}
