\title{AI4AI4Cell: An Auditable Harness for Evidence-Gated Agentic Research in Cell Biology}
\author{Anonymous}
\date{}
\maketitle
\begin{abstract}
We study whether structured research loops and local biological diagnostics improve executable models under auditable evaluation contracts. AI4AI4Cell connects hypotheses, versioned code, fixed evaluators, retention decisions and subsequent proposals. Its federated-evidence interface lets partners evaluate artifacts locally and return bounded diagnostics while the research language model remains fixed. We evaluate five tasks across cell policies, drug--target interaction prediction and observed-response proteomics. Exploratory cell results show small harness gains but no additional same-data federation benefit. In a five-seed native DTI comparison, our harness attains unseen-drug AUROC 0.8922; its differences from DrugEvolve and direct generation are positive but uncertain. A prospectively frozen proteomics study crosses direct/loop research with pooled/client-resolved evidence across 32 campaigns, holding training, partner observations and the numerical selector fixed. All 42 deployment records are evaluated after selection is sealed. Our harness with client cards attains AP 0.1652 versus 0.2002 for direct generation with the same cards, a paired difference of $-0.0349$ (Holm $p=0.125$). Neither primary contrast supports a loop or diagnostic-package advantage; logistic regression also outperforms the neural methods on AUROC and log loss. Complete decision chains and 128 successful fits establish executable traceability, while the measured outcomes expose its separation from predictive utility. Local-data execution is not a formal privacy guarantee.

\end{abstract}
